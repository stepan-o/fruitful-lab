Nine craft interviews

Nine specialists answered seven fresh questions each: 63 answers. Their complete answers are retained here, including their own failures, tradeoffs and disagreements. This round was written separately from Root’s account and the external systems assessment. Writer’s author-issued W4.2 revision supersedes its earlier audience recommendation; the earlier version remains in private provenance.

Q6: primary proposed improvementAnswersRoles
Review an integrated encounter before expanding or polishing it7Engine, Level, Animator, Director, Visual, 3D Artist, Critic
Compare the new mechanic with a matched case without it1Systems
Check essential recipients, knowledge and hearing before script handoff1Writer

These are manually classified primary proposals, not votes for one detailed workflow. The coding uses each role’s explicit Q6 proposal once. Systems asks whether an addition earns its cost; Writer asks whether a later action depends on information that was actually received. The seven encounter proposals differ in ownership and emphasis—composition, contact, causal staging, technical state or independent first impression.

Viewing coverage: all nine Q4 answers disclose still or sampled-frame inspection; none claims continuous playback. This limits the review rather than cancelling the visible findings. A persistent sampled pose or covered face is observable; fluidity, grip, impact and sustained pacing need different evidence.

There is no case for turning those suggestions into an approval ritual for every asset. Level and Critic explicitly reject that tradeoff. Visual accepts a separate reading rail’s weaker spatial connection to the speaker in exchange for stable acting space. 3D finds no basis for a blanket model rebuild. Systems would rather omit an unconvincing second visit than defend it with more subsystems. The current plan retains those distinctions.

The interview round also records bounded mistakes: Critic missed adjacent drawing defects, Visual’s anchor-first recommendation was too narrow, 3D framed a local geometry diagnostic as a finish line, and Writer revised an initial hearing recommendation after checking downstream cues. Those accounts are preserved in the full answers; they are not folded into a claim that every role made the same error.

The Producer’s reflection makes its own delivery decision from this evidence. The separate agentic-systems assessment will evaluate that account after it is frozen; the native game Critic’s interview does not substitute for that assessment.
