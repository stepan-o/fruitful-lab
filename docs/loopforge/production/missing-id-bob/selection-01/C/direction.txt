Ink and oxide

Graphic industrial etching with true drypoint crosshatching in plate shadows, a restrained transparent rust wash and hand-tinted cyan glass. Combine independent broad silhouettes, fine marks concentrated near faces and bare handoff, near-black ink around the gate hinge. Warm paper should not show as a literal page. Faces, gate and empty hand remain immediately legible without text. No bright comic book contour or poster panel.
