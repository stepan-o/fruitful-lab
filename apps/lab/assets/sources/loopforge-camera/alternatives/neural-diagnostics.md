# Loopforge / Neural Diagnostics

Review alternative only — not selected or implemented. Generated 8 October 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. One original 1536 × 1024 PNG, with no image post-processing or corrective rerun.

## Direction

Worn ivory enamel gives the brain factory a disquieting clinical origin, linked to Cathexis's porcelain construction. Black smoked glass keeps sealed rooms truly unreadable. Copper links machine anatomy to the factory without brass ornament. The cast's dark recognizable faces remain strong against their pale fittings.

The sheet combines an off camera monitor, two identity-preserving portrait sockets with blank speech balloons, a commit button, four resource concepts, seven palette swatches and three material studies. The monitor deliberately reveals nothing behind its black glass. “LATTICE FORGE” remains visible on the guard's handwritten tape label.

- Aged bone enamel: `#CFC5AD` — Main equipment panels and fittings.

- Desaturated cool cyan: `#71BCC3` — Narrow chassis lamp and Limen eyes.

Authored direction targets, not measured samples or final runtime accessibility tokens.

## Type proposal

- Instrument labels: Narrow utilitarian sans-serif capitals with squared terminals, moderate tracking; engraved or stamped finish restricted to large static labels.
- Live reading text: Plain humanist sans-serif, medium weight, normal case, clean without distress. Final family should use existing self-hosted project fonts or a reviewed compact subset.
- Tape labels: Original irregular dark-pen handwritten capitals; image treatment only for fixed room names, with a matching accessible DOM label in a future implementation.

Compact instruments retain apparatus character while dialogue and operational labels stay readable.

## Tradeoffs

- Pale enamel increases immediate silhouette and label contrast but can dominate a six-camera wall; larger implementation should reduce repetitive edge damage and preserve dark reading gutters.
- Fine cracks and screws reward close inspection but must be simplified for phone-size components.
- Speech balloons are intentionally blank and compact in this style sheet; actual live dialogue needs separate responsive sizing and semantic text.
- The DELIVERY concept shows a crate; the weekly deadline needs an adjacent factual text label in an implementation.
- This sheet defines material and component language, not a complete gameplay layout, final font choice or production-ready asset atlas.
