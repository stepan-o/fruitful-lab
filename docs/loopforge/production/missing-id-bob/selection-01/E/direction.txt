The held line

Observational editorial oil-and-ink painting with brushed matte planes and sparsely placed, sharp accents. A band of cool light on the stopped workers gives an immediately readable procession from jo and gus to the lower gate and the empty counter. The practical light breaks up again around bob's open hand. LIMEN has quiet weight and presence in the foreground. Desaturated Prussian blue and muted ochre. Do not shift the action or enlarge the crowd.
