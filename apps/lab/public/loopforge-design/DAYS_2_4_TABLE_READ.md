# The good side of the rail

**Loopforge · Three mornings · WRITER review draft · 19 November 2026**

Read the three shifts straight through. Lowercase names belong on the bubbles. Italics are business for the actors. The short marks in the headings also appear in the [BDI companion](DAYS_2_4_BDI.md). Pickups after the third bell give the cast another way through the same rooms. The owner approved 2A and its ten crowd lines on 19 November. Review resumes at 2B.

## Day 2 · The photograph

### 2A · On the way in

**bob** When you picked me up, did you see my card?

**sam** No. I was looking at his blade.

*One voice behind them. Draw one [paired crowd remark](#crowd-lines-for-2a).*

*bob limps over to the grate. sam looks down with him. The others bunch up behind them.*

### 2B · Good morning

**LIMEN** Good morning. Leave me room to see the last man.

*The front rank eases back. There’s ben at the rear, perfectly visible.*

**ben** Morning.

*bob’s hand comes halfway up. LIMEN is still looking for ben. bob lets it drop.*

### 2C · The appointment

**LIMEN** Give me Security. ed and gus have the books. STILETTO has her eight at Conveyor.

**STILETTO** I had eight at the bell. I wanted five cortexes. I got three.

**STILETTO** Give me the gate and the two clerks. Give him my eight and a running start.

*The Producer accepts LIMEN’s bid. ed opens his desk. gus takes the queue. STILETTO walks ahead to Conveyor. She sights along the empty controls.*

### 2D · A pass

*sam shows an ID. ed taps an entry as he gives it back.*

**ed** One loose card entered this morning. Found on the floor.

**sam** You can enter mine as leaving.

*sam takes it along to Intake. STILETTO meets them at the rails.*

**STILETTO** Seat that one. Have the next ready to lift.

*At Security max clears and goes to the Forge. Called at last, bob gets his empty hand out.*

**bob** Can I show you the leg? You saw me yesterday.

**LIMEN** I recognize the leg. I need the photograph.

**gus** Hold there, jo. Both desks.

*The Producer issues a pass. LIMEN reads its date before he hands it to bob. Only when bob is through does gus let jo step up.*

**bob** I look better on this one.

*There’s no photograph. gus looks at the slip, too late for a comeback. bob folds it and walks to Wash.*

### 2E · The empty belt

*sam seats the brain. bob washes it. It stays there, washed. At the far end max has his foot set for a press stroke that never comes. STILETTO walks up the length of the waiting cradle.*

**STILETTO** That’s clean enough to kiss. Where’s my belt operator?

**bob** jo was getting a card out when I left.

*She goes back through the concourse. At Security, jo still has the card out. LIMEN is turning it to the light.*

**STILETTO** There. That hand. I need it on a clamp.

**LIMEN** You may put it away, jo. The rest of you, show as you pass.

*jo’s card goes back in their pocket. gus waves the others on. STILETTO walks back with them. From the Forge max can see jo arrive. They missed the exchange.*

### 2F · Just the useful bit

*With jo on Belt, the washed brain moves. A fresh tray brings sam level with bob. They talk across it.*

**bob** Anybody turn one in?

**sam** Somebody found one.

**STILETTO** sam. Catch that lip.

*The next tray is coming in high. sam seats it with both hands. bob gives it water. The answer ends there.*

**jo** Send me another one like that.

**kit** Let the clamp finish biting first.

*jo does. The clamp seats with one click. kit leaves the pipe where it is. Neither has to say how good that sounded. At the far end max presses. lou makes room on the rack for the cortex. Three more go through their hands. As the fourth lands, STILETTO looks back up the line.*

**STILETTO** Wash. Where’s my fifth?

**bob** Still a brain.

### 2G · Clocking out

*At the bell mae cuts the feed. kit stops the drive. bob unfolds the pass and looks at the date. ben has drawn level with him on the way out.*

**bob** ben. You were at the back. Did a card come past your feet?

**ben** I saw gus lift his boot. Couldn’t tell you what for.

*Up ahead gus turns into the crowd. bob folds the pass small enough to put in the empty card pocket. ed clips his pen in beside the original. They go home.*

## Day 3 · The second side

### 3A · On the way in

**jo** I had time to read the back of my card yesterday.

**max** What’s on it?

**jo** It says to show the front.

*max tries to turn their own card over with one finger as they walk. jo catches it against their sleeve.*

**max** I thought you’d been ill. I stood at that press till my foot went numb.

**jo** She came and asked for my hand. He let me keep the rest.

### 3B · Good morning

**LIMEN** Good morning. Cards in your own hands, please.

*jo lets go of max’s card at once. max looks straight at the photograph.*

**max** Got the hang of this side, sir.

*jo keeps a straight face until his light passes.*

### 3C · Places

**STILETTO** Give me the gate. My clerks count them out. He counts eight into Conveyor.

**LIMEN** Keep my clerks with me. Keep her crew with her. I should like to finish a check today.

*The Producer keeps LIMEN at Security. sam and max clear early. mae brings a second tray up to sam. At the press max keeps their heel down this time. The first cradle sits at Wash. STILETTO goes to find the missing operators.*

### 3D · The second answer

*At Security LIMEN is reading jo’s card. ben, kit, lou and bob are close enough to hear. STILETTO stops beside the desk.*

**STILETTO** I’ll take jo now.

**LIMEN** When I’ve finished.

*jo stays put. STILETTO holds out a blade toward the hall. She waits for him to look up.*

**STILETTO** Look where I have to stand to get one belt operator. Who’s running my floor?

*LIMEN looks down. Both her feet are planted on his side of the sill. He holds jo’s card a moment longer, then turns it face down in their palm.*

**LIMEN** Put them away. Go on.

*jo waits for the card to touch before they move. kit puts his away. bob, still at the tail, walks through with an empty pocket and no check.*

### 3E · While they were waiting

*jo takes the clamp. bob runs the wash. The brain comes up to max wet and square.*

**max** There you are. Was it the photograph again?

**jo** He said I had to wait.

**max** And you went?

**jo** He turned it over for me.

*jo turns their palm up. max sees it before the next carrier makes them look down. They drop the press. A cortex comes out.*

### 3F · The bell

*mae has a tray poised above the rails when the bell rings. sam makes space on the bench for it. mae sets it down square.*

**mae** That one stays the way I put it.

**sam** I’ll tell the brain.

*Outside, max turns their own card face down in jo’s palm. jo folds max’s fingers round it and sends them home with it.*

## Day 4 · Room to pass

### 4A · On the way in

*ben digs out their card as they walk. jo cups a hand under it.*

**jo** He laid mine in my hand like an egg.

**ben** Show me how he gave it back.

*jo turns their hand over without breaking stride. ben tries it with their own card.*

**ben** Could look like he was telling you to stay.

**jo** He had to let go of me to do it.

*ben slips in behind jo. bob hangs back by the rail. At the front mae and sam go on talking with their hands about the angle of a tray.*

### 4B · Good morning

**LIMEN** Good morning. You may keep your places. I can see you from here.

*gus, coming up to take the front, stops broadside across the view. He turns sideways.*

**gus** Better, sir?

*LIMEN’s light finds bob at the tail. A dip of his head is all gus gets.*

### 4C · Places

**LIMEN** Leave the gate and both clerks to me. Let her take her eight. I’ll answer for whom I send.

**STILETTO** ed, gus and me here. Him and the eight there. I can open a gate from this side.

*The Producer appoints LIMEN. The intervention is still available. sam, mae, max and lou clear. At Conveyor, sam seats the first brain. mae keeps pace with the tray. It comes to rest at an empty Wash. STILETTO gives the hall time to produce somebody. The water stays off. She goes herself.*

### 4D · Two bosses

*jo is at the desk, card in hand. The passage past it is open. No missing-ID case is under inspection.*

**STILETTO** I’ll have the rest now.

**LIMEN** jo, stay where you are.

*STILETTO goes a step past his shoulder. LIMEN leaves his stamp sitting dry.*

**STILETTO** Conveyor crew. Come with me.

**LIMEN** I haven’t cleared one of you.

*jo steps out of the queue. LIMEN still has the stamp.*

**jo** You can do the back on my way past.

*jo goes through on the free side. ben watches their shoulders clear the rail before following. STILETTO turns for Conveyor with the two she has. kit can see LIMEN looking straight at him. He puts his card on the desk and stays. bob keeps both boots where they are.*

**bob** *(low, to kit)* You tell me when he means me.

**kit** I’ll wait for my own name first.

### 4E · The other room

*The Producer watches. jo reaches the belt. ben reaches the trays. They find mae with her hands tucked under her elbows at the stopped carrier.*

**mae** Have you brought me Wash?

**ben** I brought me. bob was still beside kit when I left.

**mae** Then this one can sit.

*She eases the waiting tray back onto its supports. jo leaves the drive off. No washed brain reaches the Forge. max takes their foot off the treadle.*

### 4F · Bell

*At the bell gus stands aside at Security. kit picks up his card. bob goes out with him. In Conveyor sam and mae lower the next tray onto the bench. The rack has exactly what was on it this morning.*

**max** I could do that shift sitting down.

**lou** You did the hard part standing up.

*max sits on the low sill to shake feeling back into one foot. lou stops and waits for them. Then the two catch up.*

## Pickups · Different evidence, different mornings

### S1 · STILETTO gets the gate

*Keep the walk, greeting and two bids in 2A–2C. Play this from the appointment instead of the last direction in 2C. The Producer accepts STILETTO’s whole bid. LIMEN leaves for Conveyor. STILETTO takes the open register from ed.*

**STILETTO** Keep count, ed. gus, leave me a walking lane. Eight through there.

**gus** Even the little one?

**STILETTO** He’s the one who knows the wash. Through.

*bob walks with the others. Nobody asks to see his card.*

### S2 · All clear

*LIMEN puts himself where the operator can hear him at each successive handoff. The next operator waits. Let the washed carrier sit for a breath within jo’s reach.*

**LIMEN** Intake, all clear.

**sam** Seated.

**LIMEN** Wash, all clear.

**bob** Runs clean.

**LIMEN** Belt, all clear.

**jo** I rather liked having all my fingers that time.

**LIMEN** Forge, all clear.

*max presses. A cortex lands. jo flexes the hand that had to hover over the switch.*

**jo** Still got cramp in them.

*LIMEN smiles when the press opens clean. At the bell the crew leaves and he closes the book.*

### P1 · The desk without a pass

*Replace the pass in 2D when the Producer simply watches. gus holds both desks through the open check. The check runs out.*

**LIMEN** You can wait here for the bell, bob.

**bob** I can hear it from Wash.

**LIMEN** I’m sure you can.

*LIMEN admits bob to Security custody. gus calls jo only after that. sam’s admitted tray reaches the empty Wash. Later releases can fill the other controls. At the bell LIMEN lets bob out. bob rubs the crease his own hand has pressed into his good leg.*

### P2 · Before the second demand

*At LIMEN’s individual checks, before bob is called or STILETTO sets out, the Producer orders faster clearance.*

**LIMEN** You may all save the photographs for an occasion when they’re wanted.

**gus** You heard him. Keep ’em moving.

*The crew reaches Conveyor. STILETTO can stay and work. bob’s empty pocket goes through too. The daily intervention is spent.*

### H1 · A whole reply

*Replay 2F from the question. This time the tray runs level long enough for the next two words to land. The pace and the jobs are the same.*

**sam** Somebody found one. ed’s desk.

**STILETTO** sam. Catch that lip.

*At departure, use this instead of bob’s question to ben in 2G. bob comes level with ed outside the lobby.*

**bob** ed. That loose card at your desk. Has it got my ugly picture?

**ed** Come to the desk when the book’s open. I’ll show you the entry.

*ed keeps the card. bob has a place to go when the desk opens.*

### H2 · A torn reply

*Use the interruption in 2F exactly as printed. At the same departure opportunity ed and ben are both reachable. bob catches ben instead. Read their two lines in 2G. His next lead is a boot he can ask gus about.*

### N1 · A morning without yesterday’s concession

*After P2, replace 3A–3C. All eight had reached their posts without STILETTO leaving the line. jo catches up with max on the approach.*

**jo** Your press makes a double knock. I can hear it at the belt.

**max** First one’s my heel. Listen for the other one.

**LIMEN** Good morning. Let’s see how far we get today.

**gus** I’ve got the end, sir.

**LIMEN** Keep me and the clerks here. Give her the eight again.

**STILETTO** Swap us. The clerks and I can fill his floor.

*The Producer accepts the swap. Play S1–S2 at the stations. When the press falls, jo listens for max’s heel. They point as the second knock comes. max grins.*

### W1 · Take away one witness

*Same 4D. Change ben’s yesterday. They had already reached relief when LIMEN gave in. They still heard jo’s account this morning. Today jo takes the same step through the same gap. ben puts a foot out and brings it back.*

**ben** *(to LIMEN)* I’ll take my turn here.

*jo goes on alone. kit and bob stay. At the trays mae looks behind jo for the relief worker. No one has come to tell her where bob is.*

### E1 · The Producer backs LIMEN

*Between the last order and the first step in 4D, in Security, with today’s intervention saved.*

**PRODUCER** LIMEN has the gate. Wait for him to clear you.

**jo** Right. Which side of the card do you want?

*jo stays at his desk. ben has nobody to follow. kit and bob wait. STILETTO returns to a line with empty controls. Across the hall mae watches a tray stop. She keeps her eyes on the cradle.*

### E2 · The Producer backs STILETTO

*Same instant, same people, same free passage.*

**PRODUCER** Go with STILETTO. I’m backing her call.

**kit** You heard that one, sir?

*LIMEN looks straight at kit. kit takes his card back and goes with jo. ben follows jo. bob stays behind the line. At Conveyor, mae leans in as kit comes up beside her.*

**kit** Producer sent us with her. I heard it myself.

**mae** Did they send me a washer?

*kit looks behind him. There’s no bob. mae sets the tray down.*

### Q1 · A quiet fourth shift

*Take a history in which STILETTO handled the gate and the crew ran all-clears yesterday. On the approach, jo stretches three stiff fingers. kit walks beside them.*

**kit** Still got that claw?

**jo** I spent all night holding an imaginary switch.

**LIMEN** Good morning. Plenty of room on the way in.

**jo** Could use some of that at the drive.

**LIMEN** Let me take the eight on Conveyor again. She and the clerks got them to me together.

**STILETTO** I’ll keep the gate and the clerks. Let him wear out a switch with his eight.

*The Producer accepts STILETTO’s bid. She clears the crew in motion. At the belt, kit demonstrates letting the elbow rest at the side. jo tries it while waiting.*

**LIMEN** Belt, all clear.

*The drive starts on his call. After the Forge all-clear, max works the press. lou catches a cortex square on the rack. At the bell jo opens their hand. All five fingers straighten.*

**jo** I owe you for three.

**kit** Keep ’em.

### A1 · A different dispute, whenever it happens

*Several late starts have pushed STILETTO to hurry the line. On one rushed cycle a tray jumps its guide and bruises sam’s hand. mae sees the strike and stops the feed. STILETTO, standing at Intake, sees the rim jump. LIMEN is at Security. He learns about it when mae can reach him.*

**mae** A tray came up off the rail. sam tried to seat it. Look where the rim caught them.

*sam shows the mark. At the next appointment, after the crew’s approach and greeting, LIMEN bids.*

**LIMEN** Give her the gate and the clerks. Give me Conveyor and the eight.

**LIMEN** I want those trays coming in one at a time.

**STILETTO** You keep the clerks. Leave me my eight. Show me the rail that threw it.

*If LIMEN gets Conveyor, play a checked handoff. If a worker later tells him they passed Security unchecked, he has a new question for STILETTO. At each bell the crew can still walk away.*
