## Blocking framing changes

1. **Move the existing Loopforge/Root introduction before ThreeLoops.** The first encounter with the diagram currently arrives before the reader knows why a factory, game, and player are present. The caption begins “The case” and the instrument refers to the delivered slice and six controls. Moving the paragraph solves the prerequisite without adding exposition. Recommended order: the two general opening paragraphs → Loopforge introduction → ThreeLoops → short agent definition. The general purpose remains the opening, and the case is explained before it is used.

2. **Open Release on Completion, not Authority.** The chapter’s next claim concerns a correct output count paired with an implausible action. The supplied capture instead opens on Ed and a card, whose narrative importance is not explained until much later. Make completion the first/default state so a reader immediately sees why state and presentation need separate examination. Keep custody and persistence available as secondary examples.

3. **Do not call custody an authority boundary.** The current Authority state says “An object crosses an authority boundary,” but its actual evidence establishes possession and incomplete knowledge. Acquiring another person’s card does not establish acquisition of their permission or authority. That distinction matters especially before chapter 2. Use a label such as “Recorded state” or “Custody” and a heading that says what the record establishes. This is a conceptual correction, not a preference for different terminology.

4. **Finish removing chronology from the inner scene.** The outer controls have changed, but `Workshop` still labels the images “CHECKPOINT / CARD TRANSFER,” “PRODUCTION / OUTPUT THREE,” and “NIGHT / TWELVE DEPARTURES.” The last count has no job in this chapter. Use custody, completed output, and persistence labels instead. Likewise, change the nested “CASE STUDY · THREE OBLIGATIONS” to “RECORDED OUTCOMES” or equivalent: there are already three top-level views, so the second unexplained trio confuses the hierarchy. The caption’s “day is compressed into selected moments” can now become a statement about schematic state/body views and recorded timing values.
