## Publisher Guardrails

- Keep `Meaning context, not plant ID.` visible on comparison graphics.
- Do not turn the visuals into plant identification, edible-use, herbal, medical, recipe, dosage, or foraging guidance.
- Keep altar, grave, candle, and petal-path imagery warm and respectful, not Halloween, spectacle, or ritual instruction.
- The quote is a Bloom Whispers original line, not a historical or cultural quotation.
- If the site implementation copies files into `apps/bloom-whispers/public/assets/`, use the publisher-safe filenames above rather than the current working filenames with spaces and ampersands.
