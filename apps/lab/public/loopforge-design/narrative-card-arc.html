bob’s missing card

Story seeds · 21 October 2026 · Selected starting seed: ed keeps the card at Security. These are possible actions under pressure, not replacements for the approved character portraits.

What stays true

LIMEN knocks the card out of bob's hand. Another worker picks it up and keeps it. bob does not notice. Later that morning, LIMEN becomes absorbed in explaining that he struck bob for the factory's good. bob answers, “yeah, i know…” LIMEN sends him through without checking the card.

bob subsequently discovers the loss and starts his own search. The next time LIMEN runs Security, he stops bob again; the missing card brings the player into the encounter. STILETTO instead pushes workers through to Conveyor. Her indifference removes the checkpoint obstacle for that shift, not the loss or its consequences. Any recovery before a confrontation must change that confrontation.

ed — keeping it properly

ed picks up the card after volunteering for Security, before his own clearance. He puts it with the things he now considers his responsibility. bob can have it back when he asks through the desk. That distinction seems perfectly reasonable to ed, who has spent the morning waiting to be entrusted with something. It is less reasonable to bob, who has already been struck at that desk.

The trail passes through the register station: somebody saw ed collect something; gus remembers which objects came across the desk; ed knows more about the missing card than bob has told him. The return can be easy if bob submits to ed's little procedure. It can become a quarrel if bob wants an explanation first. A public player order gets the card returned but also determines how ed's new authority is treated in front of the group.

Owner-selected first production seed. The new Security role, the object and the change in ed's conduct can all be shown in the opening location.

max — a joke kept too long

max picks up the card and keeps it for a private imitation of LIMEN later, once everyone has stopped being frightened. The joke needs bob to ask for his card. When bob does ask, he is frightened again. max hears how serious it has become and says they have not seen it. Returning it now means admitting that the earlier answer was a lie.

A nearby worker remembers max trying the inspector's voice; another saw the card before it disappeared into a pocket. bob can give max a quiet chance to return it, or the lie can come out in front of the others. The card returns in either resolution; the audience, the admission and the damage to the relationship differ. Do not make every exchange a joke or let max's motive excuse the harm.

lou — a reason to come looking

lou keeps the card safe and lets bob ask around before revealing that they have it. lou likes being the person who can put a problem right. By the time bob finds them, other workers have already sent him their way. lou returns it with a small request: cover their station for a few minutes while they go to speak to someone. Whether this was kindness becomes harder to answer after the favour is called in again.

The trail is social: lou asks about bob's problem without offering the information they possess; their account of where the card was found changes in detail when another witness joins. The first request must be something bob can actually do in the factory. Recovery does not automatically create a debt; bob's response and later conduct establish whether either person believes one exists.

jo — keeping out of it

jo picks up the card while sam helps bob. They pocket it when STILETTO comes through and leave it there through the shift. Later, hearing the missing card discussed as something stolen, jo decides to put it back without becoming part of the discussion. Getting it into bob's belongings quietly is harder than handing it to him would have been. The effort to avoid a small conversation starts to resemble the theft jo resents being suspected of.

The player may see jo approach bob's station and withdraw when someone arrives. A witness remembers the pickup, without knowing what happened afterwards. bob might receive the card anonymously and continue asking who helped him, or catch jo returning it and ask why they said nothing. This is the quieter seed: it should be allowed to resolve without a large confrontation.

Seed and consequence rules

Choose a compatible holder and motive together at the beginning of the run. Do not shuffle motives freely among interchangeable workers. A later pass can give one character several plausible motives; each needs its own observed conduct and dialogue.

Fix the actual pickup, initial intention and witnesses. What happens next follows from what the people learn and do. max may confess before being confronted; ed may return the card when ordered; lou's proposed favour can be refused. The starting motive is not a command to preserve the mystery at all costs.

Keep physical custody, private knowledge, suspicions and reports separate. A pickup seen from the player's camera does not tell bob what happened. The player may know more than bob; that creates an intervention problem, not a reason to hide a visible action.

Leave evidence after offscreen actions: a witness, a changed location, a conversation referring to an earlier request. Give bob opportunities to search during ordinary work and breaks. The player can help, interfere, take a supervisor's side or leave him to it; bob must still act.

Do not award forgiveness because bob says “yeah, i know…” Do not call a shift under STILETTO a solved quest. Do not confiscate a recovered card merely to force a scheduled confrontation. The rehearsal must show ed collecting and keeping the card; an earlier jo-return action is superseded.
