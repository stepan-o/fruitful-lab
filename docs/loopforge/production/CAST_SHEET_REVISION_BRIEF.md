# Cast sheet revision brief

18 October 2026 · input-art brief for the next readability audition. No new art
or replacement models have been generated by this record. Size ratios, swatches
and STILETTO's red body panels are proposals awaiting visual selection.

Read the [cast readability standard](CAST_READABILITY_STANDARD.md) first. Existing
[Model briefs](/stepanoskin/loopforge/design#model-briefs) retain the source art and
approved results; their older images do not yet implement this hierarchy.

## Shared art request

Create modeling reference sheets for Loopforge's approved initial cast: Worker 01,
LIMEN, STILETTO and CATHEXIS. Use their current character art and approved model
views as identity references. Strengthen recognition at a small operator-console
camera scale through silhouette, larger supervisor bodies and broad, distinct
body-colour areas. Keep the industrial mechanical world, material wear and each
character's personality. Small ornamental detail is secondary to readable forms.

Start with one full-cast neutral lineup on a shared floor and scale. Workers are
the size baseline; every supervisor has a generally larger body. Audition relative
foot-to-skull heights of Worker 1.00, LIMEN 1.25, STILETTO 1.20 and CATHEXIS 1.22.
Crown and tools extend beyond that body measurement. Preserve LIMEN's broad mass,
STILETTO's long sleek limbs and CATHEXIS's theatrical dress/crown outline. Do not
normalize every figure to fill its own panel.

Use industrial-blue painted armour for workers, with dark/standard/light cohort
shades. Use light steel and graphite with cyan equipment for LIMEN; black chrome,
steel tools and a candidate broad red body accent for STILETTO; aged ivory, antique
gold and exposed dark mechanisms for CATHEXIS. Exact proposal swatches are in the
standard. Place large identifying colour areas where front, side and back views
retain them. Do not distribute every accent colour across every character.

## Deliverable views

- One lineup with common scale, floor and neutral lighting, including three worker
  shades and each supervisor. Include a worker beside each supervisor if separate
  sheets make direct scale comparison hard.
- Separate front, true side and true back neutral A-pose images for each selected
  design. Match proportions, armour boundaries, palette, costume coverage and
  attachment anchors across views. Use clean backgrounds and full uncropped feet,
  crowns and tools. Preserve clear limb separation for rigging inputs.
- An assembled-equipment sheet plus body-only rigging views where needed. LIMEN's
  emitter/energy and STILETTO's blades/welders remain modular; do not fuse glowing
  volumes or blades into the skinned body. STILETTO stays handless with forearm
  sockets. CATHEXIS retains the original face under full/half Grace and the accepted
  continuous rear fabric; do not reopen the discarded mask collection or alter
  the dress into a different costume.
- Front, side and rear silhouettes without colour, followed by a grayscale value
  lineup. These expose size/form distinctions that lighting or colour might hide.
- Small room-camera and operator-console comparison crops, mixing workers around
  the supervisors, with names and speech hidden. Show actual proposed display
  sizes, not enlarged crops presented as proof of miniature readability. Dark
  Security/Conveyor compositions are tests, not required modeling backgrounds.
- Alternate-state comparisons: LIMEN energy off/on; STILETTO blades/welders with
  flames inactive/active; CATHEXIS Grace full/half/absent and clean/dirty. Keep
  persistent identity visible when the temporary cue disappears.

## Environment comparison accompanies the sheets

Cast palettes and room palettes must be chosen together. Present the same small
cast composition against the current factory, a neutral diagnostic background,
and a proposed quieter floor/wall palette. Keep camera, pose, scale and lighting
fixed for the material comparison; compare lighting separately. Preserve the
room concept art's industrial structure and localized colour while reducing
broad green wash and noisy high-contrast detail behind moving limbs. The floor
needs enough value separation for black-chrome legs and grounded poses; making
everything darker is not a solution. Use the exact background comparison and
provisional swatches in the readability standard. No empty-room image can certify
occupied-scene legibility.

## Character-specific priorities

**Worker 01:** blue must occupy substantial torso/back and limb surfaces. Keep
mechanical hands, exposed joints and sympathetic, capable anatomy. Shade variants
belong to the same workforce, remain stable per character and can carry wear;
workers must still be readable when injured, afraid or the centre of a scene.

**LIMEN:** larger and broad, with light armour planes separating him from the
workers. His one cyan forearm energy blade remains the asymmetric dark-room cue.
Size and armour silhouette must identify him even during emitter shutdown.

**STILETTO:** larger/taller but sleek. Preserve twin blade arms, black chrome and
her red eye-level horizontal/vertical visor cut. Test red body areas large enough
to survive small views without converting her into a uniformly red robot. Keep
clear negative space around tools; welders must not erase her identity.

**CATHEXIS:** larger/taller with pale costume mass, crown and theatrical presence.
Maintain the accepted dress, back coverage, mechanical body, Grace fit and warm
eyes. Dirt can be strong and expressive while leaving a readable pale-body family.
Do not depend on a tiny smile or mask detail for distant identity.

## Handoff after sheet selection

Record selected sheet versions, scale ratios and material regions in each model's
versioned kit. Keep old accepted files as provenance/comparison rather than
silently overwriting inputs. Ask Meshy for a revised candidate from those selected
views with the same modular parts and rig-friendly pose; do not promise identical
topology or preserved skin weights after regeneration.

Normalize units and confirm body height, sockets and materials on intake. Scale
changes affect stride, reach, doors, workstations, paired contacts and blade/floor
clearance. Retarget or re-author affected motion and contact corrections, then
repeat the shared review gates and occupied-scene recognition/performance tests.
The former animation approval is a reference, not automatic certification of a
regenerated body. Archive source masters and ship measured runtime derivatives.
