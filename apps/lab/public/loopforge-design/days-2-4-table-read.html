The good side of the rail

Loopforge · Three mornings · Corrected table read · 20 November 2026

Read the three shifts straight through. Lowercase names belong on the bubbles. Italics are business for the actors. An emoji flashes over the named worker. Play the face and read only the words. The short marks in the headings also appear in the BDI companion. Pickups after the third bell give the cast another way through the same rooms. The owner approved 2A with its ten crowd lines and 2B with its sixteen greeting replies on 19 November. The adviser is appointed before the workers arrive. On Day 2 with STILETTO at Security, intercut S1 with 2A and continue directly to S2. Use 2B and 2C when LIMEN holds Security. The stage directions include the owner-approved SYSTEMS corrections. The ID-check bank serves every shift with LIMEN at Security. The worker chat bank has separate draws for the queue, neighboring posts and the walk home. Each reading below uses one possible clearance order.

Day 2 · The photograph

2P · Before the second shift ↗

Pre-shift, with the Producer. The crew is still outside.

LIMEN We have to stick to the Protocol. No need to change anything.

STILETTO Give me the gate, send LIMEN to conveyor. It took him two helpers to count to eight.

The Producer accepts LIMEN’s pitch. LIMEN takes Security with ed and gus. STILETTO takes the other eight on Conveyor. One intervention remains. Only then does the crew start its walk in.

2A · On the way in ↗

bob When you picked me up, did you see my card?

sam No. I was looking at his blade.

One voice behind them. Draw one paired crowd remark.

bob limps over to the grate. sam looks down with him. The others bunch up behind them.

2B · Good morning ↗

LIMEN looks up as the crew bunches at the grate.

LIMEN It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

Three voices together on “Loopforge.” One draw from the bank.

ed Shall I open my desk, sir?

lou (to ben) He ever say goodbye to you?

bob Morning, sir. Remember me?

ben shakes his head. LIMEN turns to the two desks.

2C · The two helpers ↗

LIMEN I think nowadays it has became increasingly common to undervalue stability.

He points to ed, then gus.

LIMEN You and you, you get to help me with ID checks again.

ed opens the register and enters one loose card found on the floor. He leaves the owner’s space blank. gus takes the other desk. One line from each clerk, one from the crowd.

ed Very sound, sir. Shall I just change the date on yesterday’s page?

gus 😎 Best gate in the business, sir. Ask anybody still standing at it.

mae (to sam) Ever seen either of them move that fast for a tray?

sam 🤭

bob catches ed’s eye. gus calls sam. ed holds out his hand for the first card.

2D · A pass ↗

Three draws from LIMEN’s ID checks. sam has the card out at ed’s desk. ed keeps his voice down over the book. sam hears him. Farther back, bob can see the pen but can’t make out the words.

LIMEN Intake, sam?

sam Intake.

ed One loose card entered this morning. Found on the floor.

sam You can enter mine as leaving.

LIMEN verifies the post. ed gives sam the checked card. At Conveyor STILETTO meets sam at the rails.

STILETTO Three yesterday. I want five. Seat that one. Have the next ready to lift.

At the desk, max is looking down at the photo.

LIMEN Chin up a little.

max raises their chin. LIMEN looks from the photograph to max.

LIMEN That’s you. Through.

max takes the card and clears the gate, second after sam. bob has jo at his shoulder in the remaining queue. gus is turning back from the open boom.

bob When mine hit the floor, could you see past sam?

jo Past sam? I could barely see sam. All I got was the back of the coat.

gus calls bob. jo stays behind the line.

LIMEN ID, please.

bob Can I show you the leg? You saw me yesterday.

LIMEN I recognize the leg. Have you got the card?

bob No, sir.

LIMEN No ID. Hold both desks.

jo hears “Hold both desks.” gus keeps them back. The Producer issues bob a daily pass. LIMEN reads its date.

LIMEN Good until the bell. Through you go.

bob (to LIMEN) I look better on this one.

There’s no photograph. bob pockets the slip and crosses the gate. He looks back. jo is getting their card out. gus calls jo forward as bob turns for Wash.

2E · The empty belt ↗

sam seats the brain. bob washes it. It stays there, washed. At the far end max has his foot set for a press stroke that never comes. STILETTO walks up the length of the waiting cradle.

STILETTO That’s clean enough to kiss. Where’s my belt operator?

bob jo was getting a card out when I left.

If bob missed the glance, skip his answer. STILETTO can go and see what is holding up the empty Belt. She goes back through the concourse. At Security, jo still has the card out. LIMEN is turning it to the light.

STILETTO There. That hand. I need it on a clamp.

LIMEN You may put it away, jo. The rest of you, show as you pass.

jo’s card goes back in their pocket. gus waves the others on. STILETTO walks back with them. From the Forge max can see jo arrive. They missed the exchange.

2F · Just the useful bit ↗

In this draw sam’s Intake position and bob’s Wash position meet at the tray rail. With jo on Belt, a fresh tray gives sam and bob a moment across it.

bob Anybody turn one in?

sam Somebody found one.

STILETTO sam. Catch that lip.

The next tray is coming in high. sam seats it with both hands. bob gives it water. The answer ends there.

jo Send me another one like that.

kit Let the clamp finish biting first.

jo does. The clamp seats with one click. kit leaves the pipe where it is. Neither has to say how good that sounded. At the far end max presses. lou makes room on the rack for the cortex. Three more go through their hands. As the fourth lands, STILETTO looks back up the line.

STILETTO Wash. Where’s my fifth?

bob Still a brain.

2G · Clocking out ↗

At the bell mae cuts the feed. kit stops the drive. bob unfolds the pass and looks at the date. ben and ed draw level with him on either side on the way out. bob turns to ben.

bob ben. You were at the back. Did a card come past your feet?

ben I saw gus lift his boot. Couldn’t tell you what for.

Up ahead gus turns into the crowd. bob folds the pass small enough to put in the empty card pocket. ed clips his pen in beside the original. They go home.

Day 3 · The second side

LIMEN Keep my clerks with me. Keep her crew with her. I should like to finish a check today.

3B · Good morning ↗

3C · Places ↗

sam and max are first through. ben is standing beside bob in what’s left of the queue. bob gets yesterday’s pass out.

bob Another one of these and I’ll need a pocket for them.

ben Can’t you use that one?

bob Read the date.

mae clears and brings a second tray up to sam. At the press max keeps their heel down this time. The first cradle sits at Wash. STILETTO goes to find the missing operators.

3D · The second answer ↗

At Security LIMEN is reading jo’s card. ben, kit, lou and bob are close enough to hear. STILETTO stops beside the desk.

STILETTO I’ll take jo now.

LIMEN When I’ve finished.

jo stays put. STILETTO holds out a blade toward the hall. She waits for him to look up.

STILETTO Look where I have to stand to get one belt operator. Who’s running my floor?

LIMEN looks down. Both her feet are planted on his side of the sill. He holds jo’s card a moment longer, then turns it face down in their palm. ben and kit see his fingers open. From the tail bob hears both orders over kit’s shoulder. The hands are hidden from him.

LIMEN Put them away. Go on.

jo waits for the card to touch before they move. kit puts his away. bob, still at the tail, walks through with an empty pocket and no check.

3E · While they were waiting ↗

jo takes the clamp between bob’s wash and max’s press. The spray stops.

jo You finished?

bob Water’s off. That’s the brain dripping.

jo Takes its time drying its hair.

The spray has stopped and the washed piece sits square. At the staffed Belt, jo takes it on STILETTO’s pace. The brain comes up to max wet and square. While the press opens, max leans towards jo.

max There you are. Was it the photograph again?

jo He said I had to wait.

max And you went?

jo He turned it over for me.

jo turns their palm up. max sees it before the next carrier makes them look down. They drop the press. A cortex comes out.

3F · The bell ↗

mae has a tray poised above the rails when the bell rings. sam makes space on the bench for it. mae sets it down square. She catches sam at the outer door.

mae That one stays the way I put it.

sam I’ll tell the brain.

In the departing crowd max turns their own card face down in their own palm, tries jo’s gesture once and puts it away.

Day 4 · Room to pass

4P · Before the fourth shift ↗

LIMEN Leave the gate and both clerks to me. Let her take her eight. I’ll answer for whom I send.

STILETTO I spent yesterday standing at his desk asking for jo. Give me the desk. He gets all ten.

The Producer takes LIMEN’s bid. The intervention is still available.

4A · On the way in ↗

ben digs out their card as they walk. jo cups a hand under it.

ben Show me how he gave it back.

jo turns their hand over without breaking stride. ben tries it with their own card.

ben slips in behind jo in either reading, after hearing the same account and watching the same demonstration. bob hangs back by the rail. At the front mae and sam go on talking with their hands about the angle of a tray.

4B · Good morning ↗

LIMEN It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

On “Loopforge,” the greeting replies come in together. One possible draw:

lou (to ben) Go on, welcome him back.

LIMEN You may keep your places. I can see you from here.

gus Better, sir?

LIMEN’s light finds bob at the tail. A dip of his head is all gus gets.

4C · Places ↗

sam clears first, then mae. Waiting beside kit at the rail, bob watches gus’s hand come up again.

bob (low, to kit) You tell me when he means me.

kit I’ll wait for my own name first.

max and lou clear. At neighboring positions on the feed rail, sam and mae raise the first tray.

mae Your thumb’s about to meet an old friend of mine.

sam Which one?

mae Little burr on the underside. There.

sam Ow. Yes. Knows me too.

sam seats the first brain. mae keeps pace with the tray. It comes to rest at an empty Wash. STILETTO gives the hall time to produce somebody. The water stays off. She goes herself.

4D · Two bosses ↗

It is late in the shift. jo is at the desk, card in hand. The passage past it is open. No missing-ID case is under inspection.

STILETTO I’ll have the rest now.

LIMEN jo, stay where you are.

STILETTO goes a step past his shoulder. LIMEN leaves his stamp sitting dry.

STILETTO Conveyor crew. Come with me.

LIMEN I haven’t cleared one of you.

jo steps out of the queue. LIMEN still has the stamp.

jo You can do the back on my way past.

jo goes through on the free side. ben watches their shoulders clear the rail before following. STILETTO turns for Conveyor with the two she has. kit can see LIMEN looking straight at him. He puts his card on the desk and stays. bob keeps both boots where they are.

kit taps his own chest, remembering what he told bob in the queue. Both stay. With STILETTO gone, LIMEN slides kit’s card toward the open register and resumes the individual check.

4E · The other room ↗

The Producer watches. jo reaches the belt. ben takes a place beside mae at the stopped carrier. Her hands are tucked under her elbows.

mae Have you brought me Wash?

ben I brought me. bob was still beside kit when I left.

mae 😑 Then this one can sit.

She eases the waiting tray back onto its supports. jo leaves the drive off. Wash is still empty when the last chance to get this piece through Belt and Forge passes. max takes their foot off the unused treadle.

4F · Bell ↗

The bell catches LIMEN still comparing kit’s card. gus stands aside. kit picks up his card. bob goes out with him. In Conveyor sam and mae lower the next tray onto the bench. The rack has exactly what was on it this morning. max and lou leave side by side. Use this exit only if the press stood idle all shift. An authorized washer arriving in time can get a piece to Forge. Play the resulting count and draw a fitting exit.

max I could do that shift sitting down.

lou You did the hard part standing up.

max sits on the low sill to shake feeling back into one foot. lou stops and waits for them. Then the two catch up.

ID checks at Security

At LIMEN’s desk, draw one whole exchange for the robot gus has called. One card, one person, one outcome. Read the names as cast in this sample. C01–C30 can be recast with a worker who has the same reason to speak. C31–C40 need the named worker and the history in the companion. All four M readings use the same missing-ID procedure.

The quiet business in every ordinary check is the same. gus calls the worker. ed finds the roster entry. LIMEN compares the original, the person and today’s assignment. On a match he marks them clear. The ID goes back into the worker’s hand and they pass before gus admits anybody else. Play any business left unspoken under the lines. The final word or gesture belongs to this worker’s check.
