# Lobby surface masters

Generated 11 October 2026 from the original owner-provided Loopforge lobby reference.
Generation briefs, source hashes and purpose are recorded in `provenance.json`.
These are diffuse surfaces, not inferred normal maps or complete pre-rendered rooms.

The immutable `loopforge-lobby` pack delivers optimized WebP variants. Babylon
holds one shared texture per surface for the lifetime of the scene. Small desk forms,
contact falloff and printed labels are authored in the geometry recipe. Moving parts
remain physical objects. Original generated images are preserved separately by the
image-generation tool; these selected source masters allow reproducible optimization.
