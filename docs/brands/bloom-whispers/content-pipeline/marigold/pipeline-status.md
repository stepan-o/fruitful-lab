## 5. Must Preserve

Core Bloom Whispers promise for this piece:

- The reader should leave with the non-generic discovery that marigold is not one flat orange symbol. It is "ritual gold" whose meaning changes by plant, culture, and placement.
- The central curiosity object is cempasuchil arranged as a path, not only as a flower to look at.
- The strongest Bloom reveal is the reported Malinalco-linked Tonalxochitl belief that yellow flowers associated with the sun held solar warmth in their corollas.

Research-backed facts that must remain:

- Cempasuchil / *Tagetes erecta* is the main Mexican marigold anchor for the article.
- *Calendula officinalis* / pot marigold is separate from Tagetes and carries many European herbal, Shakespeare, culinary, and floriography references.
- Cempasuchil is strongly tied to Dia de Muertos paths, altars, graves, offerings, scent, color, and return.
- South Asian wedding/occasion material should remain compact unless stronger country-specific research is added.
- William Morris "Marigold" is a design-history layer, not proof of ancient flower symbolism.

Bloom Whispers original value to preserve:

- "Marigold is gold with memory in it."
- "Cempasuchil is the flower that makes welcome visible."
- "Marigold does not ask you to be cheerful. It asks you to place your light well."
- The plain-language synthesis of marigold as "ritual gold" and a "placement flower."

Safety/caveat boundaries:

- Food, recipes, oil, tea, benefits, and herbal uses must stay historical/cultural, not how-to advice.
- No recipes, dosage, supplement advice, medical claims, pregnancy guidance, foraging instructions, or edible-use instructions.
- Magical/spiritual language should be framed as folklore, ritual mood, symbolism, or personal reflection, not factual metaphysical claim.
- Protection remains secondary, not the strongest verified meaning.

Pinterest/SEO intent that must remain covered:

- `marigold flower meaning`
- `marigold meaning`
- `marigold symbolism`
- `marigold spiritual meaning`
