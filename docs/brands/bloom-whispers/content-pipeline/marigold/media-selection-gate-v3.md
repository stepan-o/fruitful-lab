## Selection Principle For V3

The five-concept set should work in Pinterest search results and earn both saves and clicks.

For saves, the pin should feel useful, emotionally resonant, beautiful, or reference-worthy.

For clicks, the pin should create clear search scent plus a curiosity gap: the viewer understands the topic but still needs the article to get the full meaning.

A vertical 2:3 educational pin with one central marigold bloom or small bouquet, surrounded by five simple placement scenes: altar, path, grave, garland, and wallpaper or pattern. The scenes should feel like a warm botanical meaning map, not a classroom worksheet.

marigold symbolism
