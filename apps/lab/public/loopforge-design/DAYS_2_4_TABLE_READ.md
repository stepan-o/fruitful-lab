# The good side of the rail

**Loopforge · Three mornings · WRITER review draft · 19 November 2026**

Read the three shifts straight through. Lowercase names belong on the bubbles. Italics are business for the actors. An emoji flashes over the named worker. Play the face and read only the words. The short marks in the headings also appear in the [BDI companion](DAYS_2_4_BDI.md). Pickups after the third bell give the cast another way through the same rooms. The owner approved 2A with its ten crowd lines and 2B with its sixteen greeting replies on 19 November. Review resumes at 2C.

## Day 2 · The photograph

### 2A · On the way in

**bob** When you picked me up, did you see my card?

**sam** No. I was looking at his blade.

*One voice behind them. Draw one [paired crowd remark](#crowd-lines-for-2a).*

*bob limps over to the grate. sam looks down with him. The others bunch up behind them.*

### 2B · Good morning

*LIMEN looks up as the crew bunches at the grate.*

**LIMEN** It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

*Three voices together on “Loopforge.” One [draw from the bank](#greeting-replies).*

**ed** Shall I open my desk, sir?

**lou** *(to ben)* He ever say goodbye to you?

**bob** Morning, sir. Remember me?

*ben shakes his head. LIMEN turns to the Producer.*

### 2C · The appointment

**LIMEN** I think nowadays it has became increasingly common to undervalue stability.

*He points to ed, then gus.*

**LIMEN** You and you, you get to help me with ID checks again.

*ed opens the register. gus takes the other desk. [One line from each clerk, one from the crowd.](#the-two-desks)*

**ed** Very sound, sir. Shall I just change the date on yesterday’s page?

**gus** 😎 Best gate in the business, sir. Ask anybody still standing at it.

**mae** *(to sam)* Ever seen either of them move that fast for a tray?

**sam** 🤭

**LIMEN** I’ll keep these two at Security. STILETTO can keep her eight.

**STILETTO** Give me the gate. Put him on Conveyor with all ten. I can open a fucking door by myself.

*The Producer appoints LIMEN. bob catches ed’s eye. Before he can get a word in, gus calls sam up. ed holds his hand out for sam’s card.*

### 2D · A pass

*sam shows an ID. ed taps an entry as he gives it back.*

**ed** One loose card entered this morning. Found on the floor.

**sam** You can enter mine as leaving.

*sam takes it along to Intake. STILETTO meets them at the rails.*

**STILETTO** Three yesterday. I want five. Seat that one. Have the next ready to lift.

*At Security max clears and goes to the Forge. Called at last, bob gets his empty hand out.*

**bob** Can I show you the leg? You saw me yesterday.

**LIMEN** I recognize the leg. I need the photograph.

**gus** Hold there, jo. Both desks.

*The Producer issues a pass. LIMEN reads its date before he hands it to bob. Only when bob is through does gus let jo step up.*

**bob** *(to LIMEN, looking at the pass)* I look better on this one.

*There’s no photograph. gus looks at the slip, too late for a comeback. bob folds it and walks to Wash.*

### 2E · The empty belt

*sam seats the brain. bob washes it. It stays there, washed. At the far end max has his foot set for a press stroke that never comes. STILETTO walks up the length of the waiting cradle.*

**STILETTO** That’s clean enough to kiss. Where’s my belt operator?

**bob** jo was getting a card out when I left.

*She goes back through the concourse. At Security, jo still has the card out. LIMEN is turning it to the light.*

**STILETTO** There. That hand. I need it on a clamp.

**LIMEN** You may put it away, jo. The rest of you, show as you pass.

*jo’s card goes back in their pocket. gus waves the others on. STILETTO walks back with them. From the Forge max can see jo arrive. They missed the exchange.*

### 2F · Just the useful bit

*With jo on Belt, the washed brain moves. A fresh tray brings sam level with bob. They talk across it.*

**bob** Anybody turn one in?

**sam** Somebody found one.

**STILETTO** sam. Catch that lip.

*The next tray is coming in high. sam seats it with both hands. bob gives it water. The answer ends there.*

**jo** Send me another one like that.

**kit** Let the clamp finish biting first.

*jo does. The clamp seats with one click. kit leaves the pipe where it is. Neither has to say how good that sounded. At the far end max presses. lou makes room on the rack for the cortex. Three more go through their hands. As the fourth lands, STILETTO looks back up the line.*

**STILETTO** Wash. Where’s my fifth?

**bob** Still a brain.

### 2G · Clocking out

*At the bell mae cuts the feed. kit stops the drive. bob unfolds the pass and looks at the date. ben has drawn level with him on the way out.*

**bob** ben. You were at the back. Did a card come past your feet?

**ben** I saw gus lift his boot. Couldn’t tell you what for.

*Up ahead gus turns into the crowd. bob folds the pass small enough to put in the empty card pocket. ed clips his pen in beside the original. They go home.*

## Day 3 · The second side

### 3A · On the way in

**jo** I had time to read the back of my card yesterday.

**max** What’s on it?

**jo** It says to show the front.

*max tries to turn their own card over with one finger as they walk. jo catches it against their sleeve.*

**max** I thought you’d been ill. I stood at that press till my foot went numb.

**jo** She came and asked for my hand. He let me keep the rest.

### 3B · Good morning

**LIMEN** It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

*On “Loopforge,” the [greeting replies](#greeting-replies) come in together. One possible draw:*

**gus** TEN, SIR! Counting me.

**jo** *(low, to max)* Yours is better. You sound pleased.

**ben** Thanks.

**LIMEN** Cards in your own hands, please.

*jo lets go of max’s card at once. max looks straight at the photograph.*

**max** Got the hang of this side, sir.

**jo** 🤐

*jo keeps a straight face until his light passes.*

### 3C · Places

**STILETTO** Give me the gate. He can count all ten in at Conveyor.

**LIMEN** Keep my clerks with me. Keep her crew with her. I should like to finish a check today.

*The Producer keeps LIMEN at Security. sam and max clear early. mae brings a second tray up to sam. At the press max keeps their heel down this time. The first cradle sits at Wash. STILETTO goes to find the missing operators.*

### 3D · The second answer

*At Security LIMEN is reading jo’s card. ben, kit, lou and bob are close enough to hear. STILETTO stops beside the desk.*

**STILETTO** I’ll take jo now.

**LIMEN** When I’ve finished.

*jo stays put. STILETTO holds out a blade toward the hall. She waits for him to look up.*

**STILETTO** Look where I have to stand to get one belt operator. Who’s running my floor?

*LIMEN looks down. Both her feet are planted on his side of the sill. He holds jo’s card a moment longer, then turns it face down in their palm.*

**LIMEN** Put them away. Go on.

*jo waits for the card to touch before they move. kit puts his away. bob, still at the tail, walks through with an empty pocket and no check.*

### 3E · While they were waiting

*jo takes the clamp. bob runs the wash. The brain comes up to max wet and square.*

**max** There you are. Was it the photograph again?

**jo** He said I had to wait.

**max** And you went?

**jo** He turned it over for me.

*jo turns their palm up. max sees it before the next carrier makes them look down. They drop the press. A cortex comes out.*

### 3F · The bell

*mae has a tray poised above the rails when the bell rings. sam makes space on the bench for it. mae sets it down square.*

**mae** That one stays the way I put it.

**sam** I’ll tell the brain.

*Outside, max turns their own card face down in jo’s palm. jo folds max’s fingers round it and sends them home with it.*

## Day 4 · Room to pass

### 4A · On the way in

*ben digs out their card as they walk. jo cups a hand under it.*

**jo** He laid mine in my hand like an egg.

**ben** Show me how he gave it back.

*jo turns their hand over without breaking stride. ben tries it with their own card.*

**ben** Could look like he was telling you to stay.

**jo** He had to let go of me to do it.

*ben slips in behind jo. bob hangs back by the rail. At the front mae and sam go on talking with their hands about the angle of a tray.*

### 4B · Good morning

**LIMEN** It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

*On “Loopforge,” the [greeting replies](#greeting-replies) come in together. One possible draw:*

**mae** Morning. Can we have sam through first?

**lou** *(to ben)* Go on, welcome him back.

**kit** Heard you fine back here, sir.

**LIMEN** You may keep your places. I can see you from here.

*gus, coming up to take the front, stops broadside across the view. He turns sideways.*

**gus** Better, sir?

*LIMEN’s light finds bob at the tail. A dip of his head is all gus gets.*

### 4C · Places

**LIMEN** Leave the gate and both clerks to me. Let her take her eight. I’ll answer for whom I send.

**STILETTO** Give me the gate. Send him all ten. ed and gus know the way.

*The Producer appoints LIMEN. The intervention is still available. sam, mae, max and lou clear. At Conveyor, sam seats the first brain. mae keeps pace with the tray. It comes to rest at an empty Wash. STILETTO gives the hall time to produce somebody. The water stays off. She goes herself.*

### 4D · Two bosses

*jo is at the desk, card in hand. The passage past it is open. No missing-ID case is under inspection.*

**STILETTO** I’ll have the rest now.

**LIMEN** jo, stay where you are.

*STILETTO goes a step past his shoulder. LIMEN leaves his stamp sitting dry.*

**STILETTO** Conveyor crew. Come with me.

**LIMEN** I haven’t cleared one of you.

*jo steps out of the queue. LIMEN still has the stamp.*

**jo** You can do the back on my way past.

*jo goes through on the free side. ben watches their shoulders clear the rail before following. STILETTO turns for Conveyor with the two she has. kit can see LIMEN looking straight at him. He puts his card on the desk and stays. bob keeps both boots where they are.*

**bob** *(low, to kit)* You tell me when he means me.

**kit** I’ll wait for my own name first.

### 4E · The other room

*The Producer watches. jo reaches the belt. ben reaches the trays. They find mae with her hands tucked under her elbows at the stopped carrier.*

**mae** Have you brought me Wash?

**ben** I brought me. bob was still beside kit when I left.

**mae** 😑 Then this one can sit.

*She eases the waiting tray back onto its supports. jo leaves the drive off. No washed brain reaches the Forge. max takes their foot off the treadle.*

### 4F · Bell

*At the bell gus stands aside at Security. kit picks up his card. bob goes out with him. In Conveyor sam and mae lower the next tray onto the bench. The rack has exactly what was on it this morning.*

**max** I could do that shift sitting down.

**lou** You did the hard part standing up.

*max sits on the low sill to shake feeling back into one foot. lou stops and waits for them. Then the two catch up.*
