## Required wording corrections

### 1. Preserve the scope of the ordinary replay observation

Current: “The final package completed the day’s recorded controls and outputs; performance still failed, and exact rescue contact remained unaccepted.”

The final handoff explicitly attributes the ordinary replay—six visible controls, three outputs, all twelve workers outside at night and Ed retaining Bob's card—to the **preserved predecessor runtime**. It separately records source preservation and final two-cycle performance runs. The current sentence makes those observations sound like a single inspection of the final package. The ultimate release can still be described as playable; this is an evidence-provenance refinement, not a claim that the selection broke the game.

Suggested replacement:

> The selected runtime had reached the day’s ending in ordinary replay. The final package still failed its performance test, and exact rescue contact remained unaccepted. The release could carry those facts without issuing another automatic correction request.

Keep the predecessor/final-run distinction explicit in the supporting outcome extract even if the literary paragraph uses “selected runtime.”

### 2. State Root's actual preservation error actively

Current: “Shared diagnostic analysis and scripts were retained, but a temporary private worktree disappeared after its worker exited.”

This accurately avoids saying everything was retained, but “disappeared” obscures the recorded intervention. Root checked for tracked changes, then interrupted the worker; the temporary private worktree/folder was subsequently removed. Zero tracked diff did not establish that all private work was preserved. The evidence does **not** establish that valuable unpublished work was destroyed.

Suggested replacement:

> I had checked for tracked changes before interrupting the correction worker. That check did not inventory every private file. Shared diagnostic analysis and scripts were retained, but the temporary private worktree was subsequently removed; later unrelayed work remained unknown. “Everything is preserved” would have made the handoff sound more complete than the evidence allowed.

The paragraph can then retain its existing responsibility sentence. This gives Root the same concrete scrutiny that the chapter gives Producer, without importing Root's work into native-studio counters.
