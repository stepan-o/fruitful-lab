# Valve catalog and equipment study

8 October 2026. The developer role lists distinct released games, episodes,
engine remakes and playable shorts carrying Valve developer credit. Condition
Zero includes its Deleted Scenes companion. The Orange Box and platform ports
without distinct new content are grouped with their games. Tools, benchmarks,
soundtracks, cancelled projects and publisher-only releases are outside this
game inventory. Half-Life 2: Update and Aperture Hand Lab are separately labeled
collaborations; the latter's store credits Cloudhead, whose own account explicitly
describes collaboration with Valve. Deadlock remains an unreleased playtest.

Individual official store records were checked through Steam's public app-details
endpoint. Current package dates are not always original releases: CS2 inherits
CS:GO's 2012 listing; Day of Defeat: Source has a later platform date. Historical
announcements resolve those cases. The list is not a claim of sole authorship.

## Equipment graphic

Reference: [Valve's Steam Deck product views](https://www.steamdeck.com/en/).
Original SVG geometry identifies the device through its functional silhouette
and controls. No downloaded image, extracted model, copied game screen or altered
logo is used. The miniature is bounded editorial illustration of the equipment
role, not project branding, a retail offer or a claim of publisher clearance.
Steam Deck belongs to Valve. The existing editorial rights approach applies;
creating geometry does not erase rights in a referenced product design.

No new raster assets, dependencies or continuous JavaScript loop. Screen movement
uses CSS transforms/opacity, pauses offscreen and when the page is hidden, and
respects reduced motion and the reader's pause control. Full composition and
motion notes live in graphic-descriptions.json. Sources and paragraph citations
for the library/device argument are in company-chapters.ts.
