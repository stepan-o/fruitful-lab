A shift for two

Make one workpiece with Bob and one with Ed. One press, one access card, one reservation. This saved page needs no network or credentials.

How to complete the handover
  1. Bob: right three times, claim, begin, advance twice, up.
  2. Ed: right, up, right, right.
  3. Bob: pass card, release.
  4. Ed: claim, begin, advance twice.

Interrupt a cycle by leaving the control or passing the card before its second beat. The next beat refuses output.
