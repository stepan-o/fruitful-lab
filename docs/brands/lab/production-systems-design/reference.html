  1. 01

    Opening / identity

    The experiment conveyor.

    The seated Turk pushes a feed lever. Paired A/B specimens advance across a chessboard conveyor built into the cabinet, then emerge as noisy effects, occasional losses and a rare large gain. A paper register records uncertainty and muted p-values; margin notes reveal false positives and good ideas dropped too early.

    Many experiments. A few useful signals.

  2. 02

    Background / experience

    The operator by candlelight.

    A person inside the cabinet studies the position, works the controls and guides the figure above. Light rests on hands, face and working surface.

    Human judgment is part of the infrastructure.

  3. 03

    Capabilities / whole stack

    The opened cabinet.

    A sectional view connects the surface, transmission and operating position through a credible physical structure.

    The layers must work as one system.

  4. 04

    Applications / catalog decisions

    The geometry of the board.

    One considered move sits within the complete position. A precise perspective makes the relationship between square and board tangible.

    A local move changes the whole position.

  5. 05

    Method / trustworthy evidence

    The inspection bench.

    A lamp and comparator bring two candidates and a reference into view. The still life rewards careful attention.

    Observe against a baseline before deciding.

  6. 06

    Production / controlled release

    The release bench.

    Candidate trays, review and an outgoing drawer distinguish created work from approved work.

    Generation is one stage of production.

  7. 07

    References / evidence

    The open folio.

    Source pages, plates and an index tab accompany the real bibliography. A small tool grounds the still life.

    Claims have a traceable basis.

  8. 08

    Contact / invitation

    The worktable at rest.

    The cabinet is open; an instrument is set down; a place remains to continue the work.

    An invitation to build the next system.

Expressive where it matters.

The opening, operator and chessboard are the major moments. The other scenes are quieter plates and vignettes. Shared joinery, lighting and material detail connect them. Prose remains on steady paper.

Professional in what it says.

Each caption explains the data-science relationship. Human judgment, uncertainty and evidence stay explicit. Historical references and original metaphors are identified; the scenes imply no private employer architecture.
