## Why This Exists

The previous media gate was too abstract. A label like `Marigold Meaning Changes By Placement` is not enough to approve a visual concept.

This version gives five concrete first-round concepts by category. Each concept explains what the graphic should look like, what the overlay should say, what should be generated in ChatGPT, what should be handled in Canva, and what still needs Susy's choice.

## Pinterest Calibration Note

The Media Strategy Agent should inspect the live Bloom Whispers Pinterest account before final concepting whenever access is available:

```txt
https://www.pinterest.com/bloomwhispers1/
```

If public traction is not visible, the agent should ask Susy for screenshots, analytics, or examples of pins she thinks are working. Do not invent traction claims.

For this Marigold gate, use current Bloom Whispers direction plus a test mindset:

- keep clear search-scent pins;
- keep saveable quote graphics;
- include educational meaning graphics;
- include at least one newer editorial test;
- make room for Susy's own photos/assets if she has them.

Performance goal:

- design for **saves** by making at least some pins useful, beautiful, reference-worthy, or emotionally saveable;
- design for **clicks/outbound clicks** by making title pins keyword-led, curiosity-led, and clear about the article payoff.

## First Five Concepts

### 1. Infographic / Educational Pin

Working title:

```txt
Marigold Meaning By Placement
```

Exact overlay text:

```txt
Marigold Meaning Changes By Where The Flower Is Placed
```

Plain-English visual description:

A vertical 2:3 educational pin with one central marigold bloom or small bouquet, surrounded by five small placement scenes. Each scene has one short label:

- altar: welcome
- path: return
- grave: remembrance
- garland: celebration
- wallpaper: pattern

The design should feel like a warm visual map, not a classroom chart. Think botanical editorial card: golden marigold, deep green, candle cream, small icons or mini-scenes, clear readable labels.

What ChatGPT should generate:

- a warm editorial botanical base image with marigolds and subtle placement vignettes;
- no final text embedded, unless using placeholder labels only.

What Canva should handle:

- final overlay text;
- five small labels;
- brand typography;
- spacing, contrast, and cropping;
- any cleanup so the layout feels intentional and not AI-chaotic.

Article section supported:

- Quick Meaning
- The Simple Meaning
- Whole article thesis

Reader/search job:

Helps readers instantly understand the article's main idea: marigold is not one flat symbol; meaning changes by context.

Pinterest intent:

- `marigold meaning`
- `marigold flower meaning`
- `marigold symbolism`

Pattern status:

Core educational/search-scent asset. This should be treated as a reliable pillar pin, not a risky experiment.

Safety/culture/source caveat:

Do not make the altar/path/grave scenes look like generic Halloween decor. Keep cempasuchil/Dia de Muertos cues respectful, warm, and restrained.

Production readiness:

Ready for Production Agent prompts after Susy approves the concept.

### 2. Quote Graphic

Working title:

```txt
Not All Gold Celebrates
```

Exact overlay text:

```txt
Not all gold celebrates.
Some gold remembers.
```

Plain-English visual description:

A quiet, high-save quote pin. Close-up marigold petals or a small golden arrangement near candlelight, a paper card, a threshold, or a memory table. The image should feel still, warm, and emotionally specific. No melodrama, no funeral-stock look, no spooky styling.

What ChatGPT should generate:

- a luminous marigold still life with room for text;
- soft shadows, deep botanical green or warm cream background;
- no embedded text.

What Canva should handle:

- quote typography;
- line breaks;
- subtle Bloom Whispers signature or URL if desired;
- texture overlay or slight crop so it feels less fully AI-generated.

Article section supported:

- Bloom Wisdom
- remembrance/grief layer
- quote-card routes

Reader/search job:

Gives the article an emotional share/save asset and carries the non-generic idea that marigold is not only cheerful.

Pinterest intent:

- `marigold symbolism`
- `marigold spiritual meaning`
- flower quote / remembrance interest

Pattern status:

Current-pattern asset: Bloom Whispers can use original wisdom lines as quote pins. This one is punchier and less verbose than the article body.

Safety/culture/source caveat:

Label internally as Bloom Whispers original interpretation, not folklore or source quote.

Production readiness:

Ready for Production Agent prompts after Susy approves the concept.

### 3. Classic Pinterest Blog-Title Pin

Working title:

```txt
Marigold Meaning Pillar Pin
```

Exact overlay text:

```txt
Marigold Meaning
Ritual Gold, Memory & Welcome
```

Plain-English visual description:

A classic Pinterest article pin: full-bleed marigolds, strong title, simple subtitle, high contrast, instantly readable on mobile. This should look like a polished blog-title graphic, not an infographic. It needs clear click intent.

Image direction:

Ruffled orange/yellow marigold blooms against deep green, cream, or dusk-toned background. The flower should be recognizable immediately.

What ChatGPT should generate:

- a clean vertical marigold image with negative space for title;
- no embedded text;
- preferably one hero cluster or garland edge rather than a busy field.

What Canva should handle:

- all title/subtitle text;
- brand font pairing;
- hierarchy;
- URL/logo if used;
- export sizing.

Article section supported:

- Main article / featured pin

Reader/search job:

Gets the article clicked by people searching the broad topic. It says exactly what the post is.

Pinterest intent:

- `marigold meaning`
- `marigold flower meaning`

Pattern status:

Proven Pinterest logic / necessary baseline. Not the most innovative, but important for traffic testing.

Safety/culture/source caveat:

Do not add cempasuchil, Dia de Muertos, calendula, or source detail to the overlay. Keep this one broad and easy.

Production readiness:

Ready for Production Agent prompts after Susy approves the concept.

### 4. Comparison / Clarity Graphic

Working title:

```txt
Cempasuchil vs Calendula
```

Exact overlay text:

```txt
Cempasuchil vs Calendula
Two Marigold Meaning Layers
```

Plain-English visual description:

A two-column clarity graphic. Left side: cempasuchil / Mexican marigold / Dia de Muertos / paths, altars, memory, welcome. Right side: calendula / pot marigold / European grief, literature, herbal history. Each side gets one flower image, one short context line, and three tiny meaning labels.

This should feel like a meaning map, not a plant identification guide.

What ChatGPT should generate:

- two editorial botanical image panels or a clean split composition;
- distinguish marigold/cempasuchil mood from calendula mood without overclaiming botanical precision.

What Canva should handle:

- column headings;
- tiny labels;
- caveat line;
- layout consistency.

Article section supported:

- First, Which Marigold Do You Mean?
- Cempasuchil / Calendula distinction

Reader/search job:

Builds trust and prevents the article from sounding like every marigold means everything.

Pinterest intent:

- `cempasuchil meaning`
- `marigold symbolism`
- `calendula vs marigold`

Pattern status:

Educational test with high trust value. This is more specific than a generic quote/title pin and should be tested.

Safety/culture/source caveat:

Must include a small caveat: meaning context, not plant ID. Do not imply the graphic tells readers what is edible, safe, or medicinal.

Production readiness:

Ready for Production Agent prompts after Susy approves the concept.

### 5. Editorial / Experimental Test

Working title:

```txt
The Flower That Makes Welcome Visible
```

Exact overlay text:

```txt
The Flower That Makes Welcome Visible
```

Optional small subtitle:

```txt
Cempasuchil, memory & marigold meaning
```

Plain-English visual description:

A more cinematic editorial pin: marigold petals forming a soft path toward warm light, a doorway, or an altar-like threshold. It should feel sensory and curious, not academic. This is the mood piece that turns the article's strongest Bloom Whispers line into a visual.

What ChatGPT should generate:

- a respectful marigold petal path with warm light and negative space;
- no skull spectacle, no costume/Halloween cues, no text.

What Canva should handle:

- poetic title overlay;
- optional small subtitle;
- crop and text placement;
- subtle brand polish.

Article section supported:

- Cempasuchil Meaning In Mexico
- Paths, Altars, Graves, And Return

Reader/search job:

Creates curiosity and emotional pull. This is not the broadest SEO pin; it is the brand-building and save/share test.

Pinterest intent:

- `cempasuchil meaning`
- `day of the dead marigold`
- marigold spiritual/meaning interest

Pattern status:

Newer editorial test. Use this to avoid only repeating current graphics and to learn whether a more cinematic Bloom Whispers pin can work.

Safety/culture/source caveat:

Respectful remembrance framing only. Do not flatten Dia de Muertos into a spooky or decorative orange aesthetic.

Production readiness:

Ready for Production Agent prompts after Susy approves the concept.
