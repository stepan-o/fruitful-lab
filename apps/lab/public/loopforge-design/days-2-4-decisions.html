Who heard the second answer?

Loopforge · WRITER’s decision notes for SYSTEMS, then ENGINE · 19 November 2026

The table read follows a single family of choices. LIMEN wins all three appointments. A pass uses the Day 2 intervention. The Producer watches through the next two bells. The initials and numbers below are rehearsal marks shared with the script. Day numbers label the performances for a reader.

What the participants bring in

Interview. The authority for behavioral premises is SYSTEMS revision 16a54fc, file docs/loopforge/DAYS_2_4_SYSTEMS_STUDY.md, from “Owner interview · How Day 1 decisions work” through “How the first supervisor conflict develops.” Its companion DAYS_2_4_SYSTEMS_PLAN.md proposes the slice. Earlier scenarios in the study are historical. The approved Day One script supplies the physical starting facts and the writing guide supplies the voices. The prototype at 2dc430e has its own measured runs. In particular it starts with workers at the gate. These walk-ins, utterances, choices and resulting counts are writing proposals.

WRITER proposal throughoutThe particular jokes, bids, remembered details, social pairings, first mover, tray lip, useful two-word fragment, grip cramp and bruised hand are options for validation. Each entry below proposes how a person might choose them. The commission requires an approach and greeting on every route. The actors still need a partner, a perception and a reason to perform each one.

Shared floor rules. ed wants the book and a useful distinction to enter. gus wants the other posting and likes getting an order through to the last person. The remaining eight keep their assignments across bids. sam operates Intake, bob Wash, jo Belt and max Forge. mae stages trays, kit watches the drive, lou minds Output and ben stands by for relief. An assignment provides a destination. Travel, reaching a free control, a workpiece in the right stage, and a completed operation give a handoff its effect. An unqualified or unassigned hand at the trays gives Wash no coverage in the example. Reassignment or a lawful solo cycle can change that outcome.

How to read the entries. A named recipient below heard the delivered phrase or saw the described act. Bystanders with only a view of movement can remember the movement. Workers in another room carry on from local evidence. A called name, unsafe tray, blocked passage, completed check or bell can beat a social intention. An unfinished remark gives its recipient only the words already heard. The response can change immediately. Only personal observations, attributed reports and completed world changes are available to consolidate overnight. Run-local cues such as a raised hand expire when the hand goes down.
