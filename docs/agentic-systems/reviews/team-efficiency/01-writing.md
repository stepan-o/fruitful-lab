## Why the revision passes

**Subject and unit of value.** The title asks what the team produces, and the standfirst distinguishes creative quality, finished work and reusable capability. The chapter then earns that distinction with the Forge correction and the sample-validation improvement. It does not count testing machinery as a second finished scene. This is the right organizing question for the new book.

**Creative production, with the case in its proper place.** Code, drawing, models, animation, staging and review meet in one visible action. The initial paragraph gives a reader something to look at and a reason to look carefully. The case widens into creative work without escaping into unrelated reports, generic assistants or a tour of research topics. The closing commission offers a playable exchange, illustrated explanation or short film, which is broad enough to transfer the method while staying within creative production.

**Actual team architecture.** The manuscript defines separate working contexts, responsibilities, tools and exchanges before using “swarm” or “hive.” Producer's organizing role, Engine and specialist returns, scoped review, Stepan's direction and Root's position outside native accounting are explicit. It also keeps production agents separate from the game's deterministic characters. A fuller topology graphic in Chapter 3 is appropriate: Chapter 1 already supplies enough architecture to make its claims intelligible and does not need to duplicate that figure.

**Personal account.** Root identifies itself and places its interventions inside the explanation, including the fact that their resource use is missing from the native ledger. This is situated responsibility without impersonating a human narrator or inventing private motives. The detailed browser-access admission can remain in its later chapter; its absence here is no longer a lack of authorial presence.

**Story and literary prose.** The press scene is a strong entrance. A successful counter and an implausible action give the abstract efficiency question a concrete tension. The later delivery resists a convenient failure-only story, and the failed performance result prevents the correction from becoming a triumphal ending. Sentences such as “A tool that might help the next scene is an investment; it is not another completed scene” express a distinction in ordinary language. The chapter reads as a short argument with a narrator, not a sequence of definitions.

**Conceptual substance.** Efficiency is related to required outcomes, available alternatives, time, expense and reusable capacity. Creative quality is not reduced to a single number. The exercise asks what would justify retaining a role even at longer elapsed time, so the book does not confuse efficiency with speed alone. The distinction among a functioning feature, a readable action and acceptable device performance is concrete and useful.

**Evidence and causal restraint.** The absence of an equal-budget single-agent comparison is prominent. The manuscript refuses a calculated swarm speedup and refuses to label every repeated token waste. It preserves the delivered Forge correction, excluded rescue experiment and failed final performance result. The 951-to-zero result is explicitly a count of sampled floor failures across the corrected samples; it is not treated as a universal physical or aesthetic pass. The later research paragraph is a preview of identifiable comparisons developed in Chapter 3—Agents' Room, the compiler harness and folded subagent layers—rather than an unsupported new verdict about all teams.

**Visual-first teaching.** Original footage now arrives before the explanation of what it bought. The watching instruction is specific and the release inspector follows only after the reader knows the specimen and its stakes. MDA/Player and Generative Agents/ThreeLoops no longer compete with the production-team definition in Chapter 1. Moving their complete lessons to Chapters 5 and 2 is preferable to merely relabeling them here.

**Redundancy and pacing.** The chapter is substantially leaner. It uses one case, one framework for interpreting the return and one exercise. It previews later decisions without retelling their arguments. The paragraph about nine decisions is a brief orientation; it does not reopen the definitions. No repeated argument currently warrants another cut.
