20 November 2026 · ENGINE takes ownership of the Rust kernel

The ENGINE onboarding page records the owner’s standing decision. Every game feature runs through a shared Rust kernel with BDI character decisions and KVP-0001 at the viewer boundary. 21 November 2026 · Moving the break forward

The owner approved the Menu melody and heard too many repeats before the break. The current cut reaches the drums in 47 seconds. The solo uses short sidestick and snare strokes, toms and two quiet pedal marks. The band comes back with the full theme once, then passes its two-note tag between voices. 21 November 2026 · Letting the Menu phrase finish

The owner could hear the hook and its answers fighting for the same moment. This pass opens with the same four-bar phrase twice. Its variation takes the first three notes more slowly, arches upward and then stretches the original fall into a held C♯ over D. The lead rests before the next statement. The drummer keeps the earlier sixteen-bar feature. Hear the opening through bar 17.

21 November 2026 · Two endings and a drum turnaround

The owner found that four returns in three minutes left the Menu theme too far away. The next study brings it back after twelve bars. One reply grows out of the held C♯ and takes a different ending the next time around. In the break, the drummer drops the running hi-hat and plays across the rim, snare and toms. Two short runs mark the halfway point and the return to the tune. Dated fourth study.

21 November 2026 · Giving the Menu a second melody

The second Menu brought the drummer forward, but the owner found the returning lead too insistent and the chords too abrupt. The new opening lets the held C♯ start a second melody. The reply climbs, doubles back and later enters from a lower register while the keys ring through changes of harmony. In this third study the complete lead statement next appeared at bar 33. 21 November 2026 · Putting the Menu hook in the drummer’s hands

The owner heard a wistful Menu and lost the theme on the way to an empty drum break. Its replacement gives the kick two dependable footholds per bar. A short pulse lead plays the descent, and the drummer takes its five attacks through rims, rolls and toms before handing it back. The owner can hear that exchange from bar 49. Play the revised Menu.

A studio carried by its pages

18 November 2026 · workflow established; individual game deliveries retain their own review status.

Loopforge now proceeds through dedicated specialist chats managed by its owner. The previous autonomous studio is closed. Its output remains available, but a new chat should start from the current brief, approved design and actual artifacts rather than reconstructing a long conversation or reviving an old coordination process.

The new Studio Briefs landing collects shared instructions and six initial roles: Writer, Level Designer, Art Director, 2D Artist, Engine Master and Game Systems Designer. Each role maintains its own brief and the design page that carries its work. New specialists can be added when commissioned.

The working sequence is study and pre-synthesis, interview, approved plan, build and verification, then manual owner review. The reference book, Tynan Sylvester's Designing Games, remains standard reading. Applied concepts must have verified location references and explain the decision they informed. This workflow entry does not claim a new book-derived design result.

The current parallel commissions are the live 2D Day 1 implementation and the Days 2–4 systems study. At the time of this entry, LEVEL was commissioned to reconcile Factory floor: scale & feel with the approved layout and 2D direction. Those are assignments, not completion claims.

What each new case study records

  • A concrete problem and the exact source/build that demonstrated it.
  • The decision, alternatives and any research concept actually applied, with citations.
  • What changed in the game, asset or design and what became reusable.
  • Comparable before/after evidence where available, preferably normal-speed game video for runtime changes. Label composites, rehearsals and diagrams separately.
  • The checks actually run, the owner's review status and the remaining limitations.
  • Links back to the responsible role brief and current design handoff.

Add dated entries here and link longer studies from their specialist pages. Preserve prior results as dated evidence; do not silently replace a failed result with a later success.
