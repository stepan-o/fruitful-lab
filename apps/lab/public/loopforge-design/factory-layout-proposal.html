Factory floor proposal

Put five workroom doors behind LIMEN’s checkpoint. Give the east end of the Forge two freight shutters. One feeds Burn-in Theatre. The other lets Logistics receive basic output from the first shift.

One check, five destinations

Walk through the ID pen and its south gate. Forge is straight ahead. Turn east on the cleared apron to reach the north doors for Weaving, Brewery and Cortex, or the audience aisle in Theatre. The pen walls keep the incoming queue on LIMEN’s side of the gate. The four later-room doors can be signed and shut on Day 1.

Two arrivals through the same front door

The crew takes the solid path. max speaks on the first step, jo answers through the Lobby and bob gets his last exchange with max on the approach to Security. STILETTO takes the dashed path later. Her final approach, the open boom and the Forge staff door lie on one axis. LIMEN steps to the pier and opens the boom ahead of her. She speaks and crosses at walking pace.

Forge to Logistics with Theatre closed

lou counts the cortex onto the rack. For routine production, an adjacent fitting stand seats the cortex in a body on a wheeled pallet. A switch at the outfeed sends the pallet down the existing outbound lane. It turns east on the cross-road and passes through the lower shutter directly into Logistics. The choice at allocation sends the unit to the quota dock or to next-shift muster.

Forge to Burn-in Theatre to Logistics

A scheduled pallet goes straight east through the upper shutter. A side track takes it from Theatre receiving into a free conditioning cradle facing CATHEXIS’s stage. When its session ends, the far-side track carries the same pallet through the south shutter into Logistics receiving. Staff enter via the concourse and use the side aisle. Only units that actually attend acquire a session record.

Eight leave the line together

Six workers turn for the gate on the bell. mae cuts the feed and kit stops the drive, then they join the flow. ed ticks eight individual crossings. Everyone ahead keeps moving through Dispatch and the Lobby while ed offers the register to LIMEN. max and gus reach the yard together. ed laughs before the windows go dark.

What moves on the plan

Keep the south entrance, Lobby, Dispatch and the crane-served 80 × 56 m Forge. Widen the cleared side of Security into the central cross-aisle. Bring the south wall of Weaving up to it. Move Cortex to the north-east and bring Theatre down alongside the Forge outfeed. Logistics takes the south-east corner, where its receiving floor shares a wall with both rooms. Keep the dock on the exterior.

Dispatch supplies stillages through a receiving hatch at the west end of the Forge. Seal the old personnel shortcuts from Lobby and Dispatch into Weaving. A Logistics worker clears Security and follows the Forge service walk to a staff wicket beside the lower freight shutter. Theatre attendants have a separate wicket next to their outbound shutter. No staff door connects Security to Logistics.

Use the rack as a visible buffer. A busy Theatre holds scheduled units at receiving. When its receiving slots fill, hold them at Forge Output and stop feeding the press when the rack fills. Keep their intended destination. Release them in order when a cradle clears. Empty pallets return on the lower deck of the transfer track to the Forge empty-cradle rack. The Theatre programme, fitting stand and pallet handling are proposed staging.

At the gate

Ten workers shuffle toward Security. max has drawn his long jaw back into his collar. He lets the queue bunch up behind him before he speaks.

max It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Loopforge.

jo Privilege.

max I haven’t finished.

jo He says it’s his privilege. I’ve been coming through this gate six years. He’s never been pleased to see me.

max tries the jaw again. Something clicks behind his ear. bob leans closer.

bob How’d you do that?

max Which bit?

LIMEN It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

The jaw clicks back. bob laughs. He’s the only one still looking at max.

LIMEN Stay where you are. Who’s on his first shift?

bob Me, sir. bob.

He has his card ready. He steps out to hand it over.

LIMEN I said stay where you are.

The cyan blade catches bob across the leg. His card skids toward the grate. As sam kneels beside bob, ed stops the card with his boot and pockets it.

STILETTO Hold that gate.

LIMEN holds it. STILETTO goes straight through to Conveyor. bob gets his weight back on both feet.

bob You didn’t ask for her ID.

LIMEN Would you like me to call her back?

LIMEN lets bob through. At Intake, a brain sits crooked in its cradle.

Conveyor — the first brain

STILETTO I ask for a crew and he sends me one worker with a bad leg.

bob My leg was fine when I got here.

STILETTO Take Intake. Seat that brain in the cradle.

bob draws the loader over the cradle. His bad knee gives halfway through the stroke. He sits down hard on the motor housing and finishes the pull from there.

STILETTO leans past him to inspect the brain. It’s seated square.

STILETTO Good. Now wash it.

The cradle rolls toward Wash. bob holds up a hand. STILETTO steps over his outstretched leg and walks on beside the brain. He pulls himself up by the rail.

bob starts the spray. Black grit comes pouring out of the folds. When the water runs clear, he takes his hand off the control.

STILETTO Clamp the cradle onto the main belt. Start the drive.

bob snaps the clamp shut and hits the switch. The belt carries the brain toward the Forge.

STILETTO Last station. Compress it to head size.

bob closes the Forge press. It opens, and the first cortex slides onto the output rack.

Back at Security, LIMEN is still checking sam’s card.

Security — two volunteers

LIMEN Clear.

sam takes his card and heads for Conveyor. The queue moves up one place.

LIMEN this is gonna take all day...

He looks down the queue.

LIMEN gets one burst of four nearly simultaneous replies. lou mutters to ben as max calls out. gus comes in loud over max, and ed speaks straight to LIMEN through the noise. Let the bubbles overlap and linger long enough to read.

lou (to ben) noone...

max you, sir!

gus I DO!

ed Shall I start a fresh page, sir?

ed already has his pen out. LIMEN turns the register around for him and points gus to the other desk.

gus NEXT!

Conveyor — the second brain

bob I made that.

STILETTO Shut up and keep the line moving. You there. Intake.

sam heads for Intake. At the Forge, the press door sticks halfway open.

STILETTO Hit the door.

bob How do you know that’ll unjam it?

STILETTO I’ve stood at this fucking press, bob.

bob hits the press door with the heel of his hand. It springs open.

At Intake, sam lifts a full tray onto the feed rails and seats the second brain. bob runs the wash. By the time he starts the main belt, sam has the next brain lined up and jo has cleared Security.

STILETTO Where’s the rest of my crew?

jo Still in line. Except ed and gus. He’s got them checking cards now.

bob closes the press. The second cortex slides onto the rack. STILETTO heads for Security.

Security — the fourth check

max stands between the rails, card raised. ed has the register open. gus holds the queue. STILETTO plants herself in the Conveyor doorway and points both blades at LIMEN.

STILETTO IF YOU DON'T GET ME THOSE WORKERS IN THE NEXT 20 MINUTES I'LL USE YOUR MANURE-FILLED ROBOT BRAINS TOWARDS THE QUOTA

max turns to look. LIMEN holds the photograph beside max’s face.

LIMEN stamps the register, returns max’s card and opens the gate. max ducks under STILETTO’s blade on his way to Conveyor. LIMEN holds out his hand for the next card.

Conveyor — the crew fills in

max takes the Forge. jo takes the main belt. bob moves to Wash. At Intake, sam has the next brain seated.

Four quick cuts on LIMEN’s stamp. mae, kit, lou, ben. Each comes through the gate with a card back in hand.

mae slides a fresh tray within sam’s reach. kit rests two fingers on the drive housing. lou takes his place at Output beside the two finished cortexes. ben stands by for relief.

STILETTO comes back through the doorway. This time there’s a worker at every station.

STILETTO Run it.

One continuous shot with the brain. sam feeds it. bob washes it. jo clamps it and starts the belt. max drops the press. The third cortex hits the rack.

lou Three.

End of shift

The shift bell rings. mae cuts the feed. kit stops the drive.

At Security, ed ticks off the eighth worker and holds the register out for LIMEN.

ed All eight accounted for, sir.

LIMEN walks straight past him. ed sets the register down and clips his pen into the pocket with bob’s card.

Outside, max falls into step beside gus. His chin comes up.

max All eight accounted for, sir.

gus Do LIMEN.

max pulls his jaw back into his collar.

max WHO HERE REALLY CARES ABOUT THIS FACTORY???!!

ed laughs first. Behind him, the factory lights go out.

Build marks for the next blockout

  • At the south entrance — a working double door, the overhead factory sign and enough yard for ten staggered starts plus STILETTO’s later approach.
  • At Security — the queue rails, boom and west pier, LIMEN’s blade clearance, the two desks, register, grate and card slide. Allow sam to kneel while ed stops the card. Mark all five doors on the cleared side. Keep the short apron from the boom to Forge free for the through-pass and fourth check.
  • In the Forge — preserve the long carrier bed, rails, operator walk, overhead crane, receiving stillages, transfer road and overhaul bays. Block three rack positions, the adjacent body-fitting stand, the pallet switch and upper/lower shutters at full carrier width.
  • Inside Theatre — a receiving stop, a row of docking cradles facing the stage, the operator’s side aisle and a separate outbound track. Leave an occupied cradle in place while a second pallet arrives. Keep the screen and CATHEXIS visible to the audience.
  • At Logistics — both receiving mouths, a body-width staff wicket beside each, allocation position, two marked lanes, retained muster, quota dock and empty return. A retained unit reports next shift. A quota unit leaves the building.

For blockout acceptance, walk from the checkpoint into each unlocked workroom. Trace one identified unit across every handoff with Theatre running, closed and full. Trace a Logistics attendant on foot beside the freight. Rehearse all eight beats at 1× with simultaneous crossings, a loaded rack and a clear camera view of the card, the fourth check and the third brain.
