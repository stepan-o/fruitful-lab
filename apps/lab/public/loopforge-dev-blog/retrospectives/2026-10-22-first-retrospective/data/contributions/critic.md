# Critic — first retrospective

## Q1

**Facts.** My version-label finding produced a correction that I independently rechecked. That closed a specific documentation defect; it did not establish better dialogue delivery or scene acceptance. My earlier interview records that distinction. [C](#retro-critic-sources)

My first opening review also exceeded its allowance. I own that failure to bound the review, rather than treating accuracy as permission for unlimited checking. The saved result remained useful, but usefulness did not erase the overrun. Neither fact establishes how much faster a different review would have been. [C](#retro-critic-sources)

## Q2

**Proposed craft rule.** Give me the artifact, approved reference and acceptance question before the author's diagnosis. Save my first observations in the existing review before opening the explanation. Do not withhold necessary canon or version identity to manufacture blindness.

For a card-transfer board, I pass the custody claim only when the intended recipient visibly has the card and the alternative outcome is distinguishable at the supplied review scale. A giver still holding it above open receiving hands fails that claim, even if the caption says “returned.” A different palette is an owner taste question unless it prevents an agreed identity or action from reading. Static evidence cannot pass animated contact. The author should receive the exact panel, effect and smallest correction, plus what already works. [C](#retro-critic-sources)

## Q3

**Interpretation.** I agree with the independent assessment's warning that agreement is weaker evidence when reviewers share a supplied diagnosis. The cache allegation demonstrated that risk; the later mutable-record explanation required qualification, not a declaration that the checker was proven correct. [A](#retro-critic-sources)

Engine's fresh proposal makes a useful commitment: exercise the real handoff, including successful execution and deliberate identity rejection, without manual payload repair. I would change its failure classification. A permission denial is a capability failure; an accepted wrong identity is an implementation failure. Both stop progression, but assigning them the same remedy would recreate serial correction work. I also would not let a successful rehearsal become evidence that the next scene reads well. Engine explicitly preserves that boundary. This is agreement on one test, not consensus on the whole production structure. [E](#retro-critic-sources)

## Q4

**One prioritized experiment.** I am accountable for a two-stage review of the next authorized, single-artifact delivery. Producer supplies one acceptance question; the author separates rationale from the artifact and approved references. The smallest output is one existing review with a saved first observation and a subsequent evidence-based decision.

Inspect at the first handoff and once after one correction. Success means every required revision identifies observable evidence and gives the author a concrete next action, while supported passes remain explicit. Record whether the explanation changes the verdict and why. If the decision requires unavailable motion, audio or runtime evidence, stop that gate and request that evidence instead of expanding the prose review. This is a proposed experiment, not a restarted game task or publication claim.
