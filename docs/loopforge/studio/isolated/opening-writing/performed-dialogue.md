## The doorway

LIMEN fills the Security doorway. He addresses the arriving workers. The front stops; the others settle behind them.

<!-- D04: arrival/morning/Stop and wait/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** STOP. WAIT HERE!!

<!-- D05: arrival/morning/Welcome to Loopforge/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** Welcome to Loopforge. You move when I say.

lou lowers their voice towards ben and kit. kit answers lou just as quietly, with LIMEN still in front of them. ben gives no answer.

<!-- D06: arrival/morning/lou under his breath/say:0 -->
**lou:** Is he always like this?

<!-- D07: arrival/morning/kit keeps his voice down/say:0 -->
**kit:** Keep your voice down.

LIMEN looks over the waiting group. bob has his card ready and takes the question for an invitation.

<!-- D08: arrival/morning/A card held ready/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** IDs ready. First shift?

<!-- D09: arrival/morning/bob steps into the opening/say:0 -->
**bob:** Yes, sir. Here—

bob steps forward, holding out his card towards LIMEN. The cyan blade swings.

<!-- D10: arrival/morning/The cyan warning/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** I SAID STOP!!!!

The blade hits bob. He falls across the foreground and the card skips away. sam runs around his feet and kneels beside him, speaking to bob.

<!-- D11: arrival/morning/sam reaches him/say:0 -->
**sam:** Easy, bob. I’ve got you.

While sam supports bob, ed bends for the card. He lifts it, then puts it away. The camera stays long enough to see the transfer. bob remains turned towards sam; he does not see ed keep it.

## The exception

sam releases the supporting hand. bob begins getting up as STILETTO comes through the entrance. Her demand reaches LIMEN and the workers in her path.

<!-- D12: arrival/passage/bob gets up as STILETTO arrives/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Get the slag out of my way!

The crowd opens around her. LIMEN jumps aside. She passes him without stopping and continues towards Conveyor. bob is on his feet; sam stays nearby. LIMEN comes back to the doorway.

bob speaks towards LIMEN, with sam beside him.

<!-- D13: arrival/bobs-turn/bob notices the exception/say:0 -->
**bob:** You didn’t check her ID…

<!-- D14: arrival/bobs-turn/LIMEN hears enough/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** WHAT DID YOU SAY?!

LIMEN has turned the remark back on bob. bob gives him another sentence to accept. sam remains within the exchange.

<!-- D15: arrival/bobs-turn/bob finds a safer sentence/say:0 -->
**bob:** I said it’s great to see someone care so much about the Protocol, sir!

<!-- D16: arrival/bobs-turn/LIMEN accepts the compliment/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** Finally you’re starting to get it. ASSIGNMENT?!

<!-- D17: arrival/bobs-turn/bob names his station/say:0 -->
**bob:** Conveyor, sir.

<!-- D18: arrival/bobs-turn/bob is cleared for Conveyor/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** Go. Before I change my mind.

bob limps through Security and follows the corridor to Conveyor. LIMEN has asked his assignment and let him through; he has not checked his card. ed still has it.

## The volunteers

LIMEN looks at the remaining queue. The first remark is to himself. Then he raises his voice to the waiting workers.

<!-- D19: arrival/helpers/LIMEN considers the queue/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** Slag. This’ll take all shift to do right.

<!-- D20: arrival/helpers/Who cares about this factory?/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** WHO HERE REALLY CARES ABOUT THE FACTORY?!

ed answers LIMEN distinctly. gus follows, slowly enough to make his own offer.

<!-- D21: arrival/helpers/ed volunteers/say:0 -->
**ed:** I do, sir.

<!-- D22: arrival/helpers/gus volunteers/say:0 -->
**gus:** Me too.

<!-- D23: arrival/helpers/Two places at Security/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** You two. Help me set up this checkpoint.

ed goes to the register; gus takes the trays. They have been appointed to Security. They are not two workers released to Conveyor.

LIMEN addresses the queue. ed passes on the instruction; gus supplies the volume.

<!-- D24: arrival/helpers/IDs and papers ready/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** IDs AND PAPERS READY!!!

<!-- D25: arrival/helpers/ed repeats the order/say:0 -->
**ed:** IDs AND PAPERS!

<!-- D26: arrival/helpers/gus likes the volume/say:0 -->
**gus:** NEXT!!!

sam comes forward at LIMEN’s call and answers him, ed and gus.

<!-- D27: arrival/helpers/sam comes when called/say:0 -->
**LIMEN:** NAME AND ASSIGNMENT?!

<!-- D28: arrival/helpers/sam starts reporting/say:0 -->
**sam:** sam. Conveyor.

sam draws his own card and presents it. Leave him holding it for inspection as attention moves to Conveyor.

## One worker for a whole line

STILETTO has reached Conveyor. bob stands before her; the other workers are still at Security. She addresses him, with no one else on the line to put to work.

<!-- D29: arrival/intake/One worker for a whole line/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** One worker. He sends me ONE.

<!-- D30: arrival/intake/bob offers to start/say:0 -->
**bob:** Where do I start?

<!-- D31: arrival/intake/The intake instruction/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Intake. Seat the brain in the cradle.

**Seat the brain.** bob reaches the Intake control and runs the loading mechanism. The dirty brain settles into its carrier. STILETTO moves along the line and directs him towards Brain Wash.

<!-- D32: arrival/wash/The wash instruction/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Wash it. We sell thoughts, not yesterday’s grease.

**Run the wash.** bob follows to the control and runs the cycle. The same brain comes out clean. STILETTO continues to the next station.

<!-- D33: arrival/belt/The belt instruction/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Main belt. Clamp it down. Start the drive.

**Start the main belt.** bob reaches the control. The clamped brain travels down the line.

<!-- D34: arrival/forge/The Forge instruction/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Lattice Forge. Close the press. Head-sized.

**Compress the brain.** bob operates the Forge from its control. The platen lowers; one smaller cortex comes out. He turns to STILETTO.

<!-- D35: arrival/first-output/bob sees the empty stations/say:0 -->
**bob:** I’m all four stations?

<!-- D36: arrival/first-output/STILETTO points to the queue/say:0 -->
**STILETTO:** Until the rest of my shift gets past that tin god.

At Security, LIMEN completes sam’s check. sam stows his own card, lowers the empty hand and walks through to Conveyor. He is the only worker released after bob during this lesson. ed and gus remain at their stations; bob’s card remains with ed.
