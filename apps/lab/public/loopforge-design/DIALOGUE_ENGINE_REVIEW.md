## Decisions to return

Confirm the meaning of the second clearance. This draft uses the second distinct completed Security passage by a worker bound for Conveyor. An ID verified while its bearer is still at the desk, a detention, a second scan of the same worker and a second finished cortex do not advance that count. If the intended event is the second arrival at a Conveyor post instead, move the opening to that event and repeat G1 with the same queue.

Settle which occupied work positions count as adjacent and when a relief worker counts as positioned. The reference casting assumes Intake can stand beside Wash, the drive beside Belt, and helpers can be stationed beside the tray or rack operator. Validate the actual room geometry and role compatibility before accepting a dialogue that relies on any of those pairs. Recheck distance and attention when a reply would be delivered.

Rule on spending at the first delivered phrase, what counts as a fresh pause for Conveyor’s second slot, and the tie-break for bob when several workers want to speak. The draft counts a real worker reply on the walk in towards bob’s daily social aim and still lets the missing card motivate another attempt. A visible, intentional reply gesture can count socially without delivering a card fact. Settle whether an incidental bystander can identify a private speaker through machinery.

## What ENGINE needs after validation

Record the shift, slot, opening event and remaining budget. Log the shuffled queue, actual passages, posts, coordinates and arrival times. Identify the initiator, intended listener, other witnesses and who heard each phrase. Save the eligible topics, reasons, selected exchange and seed. After an interruption, record the words delivered, bob’s progress toward a reply, next intended contact and each resulting personal belief with its source. Compare item transfer separately. Replaying or restoring an interrupted shift must not spend a slot or add the same observation twice.

Return each contested rule with **accept**, **revise** or **reject**, the test ID, the observed line or action that decided it and any changed admission condition. WRITER can revise dialogue whose motives fail the read. ENGINE takes the accepted rules and supporting traces.
