## Changes already established by the owner

The originals were written for an earlier, report-led prototype. Their creative purpose survives, but several implementation prescriptions no longer describe the intended game.

| Earlier prescription | Current owner direction | Practical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The report or narrative lens is the principal deliverable. | Interactions must be legible in the 3D world; speech belongs to people in that world. | Judge the scene without relying on a diagnostic explanation. Reports remain useful production tools. |
| Everyone can be a protagonist. | bob is the continuing protagonist within a substantial ensemble. | Preserve a life the player can return to, including pleasure, routine and friendship. Do not give all ten workers equal foreground in every shot. |
| Psychological memory and emotion only shade summaries and never affect behavior. | BDI-driven agents perceive, remember, act and receive feedback. | Systems and Engine must specify how remembered experience can affect a later decision. Do not claim this is already implemented everywhere. |
| Supervisors can be represented as an external pressure signal. | Supervisors are embodied actors: they obstruct, travel, speak, work and collide. | Ground pressure in particular actions and opportunities. LIMEN holds workers; STILETTO demands labor and output. |
| The observer can inspect everything. | The player has incomplete attention and power, increasingly across six rooms. | Preserve full developer evidence while limiting player and character knowledge. A missed event can leave an attributable trace. |
| The doctrine forbids randomness; a later section proposes seeds. | Card holder and motive may vary by seed; the first preview uses ed. KVP specifies reproducibility from seed and inputs. | Keep the source disagreement visible. Use explicit, recorded seeded choices where approved; never improvise contradictory outcomes for a shot. |
| Failure and breakdown dominate the framing. | The factory must be interesting to inhabit and watch across longer arcs. | Leave room for competent work, relief, humour and affection. Pressure does not require inevitable misery. |

These distinctions are grounded in the owner's instructions in this chat and the current [This is bob](<${ENGINE_WORKTREE}/docs/loopforge/CHARACTER_DRAMATURGY.md>), [Writing Loopforge](<${ENGINE_WORKTREE}/docs/loopforge/EDITORIAL_VOICE.md>), [current opening script](<${ENGINE_WORKTREE}/docs/loopforge/OPENING_EPISODE.md>) and [current performance and time contract](<${ENGINE_WORKTREE}/docs/loopforge/production/DAY_ONE_ARRIVAL.md>). The proposed briefs apply them without silently editing the originals.

## KVP belongs to Engine Master

[KVP-0001](<original-loopforge/docs/sim5/SOPs/KVP-0001.md>) defines the historical kernel/viewer boundary. Its companion Semantics, Canonicalization & Hashing and Compliance Checklist preserve the supporting contract. The “Million-dollar plan” is a historical implementation plan and must not be treated as normative merely because it is adjacent.

Engine Master owns the applicability and migration assessment against current code. That assessment should say which existing guarantees remain, which adapters or versioned changes are needed, and which legacy runtime assumptions no longer apply. This packet archives the full files but does not claim a completed KVP audit or certify present compliance.

Visual Artist, 3D Artist, Animator, Director and Level Designer supply concrete delivery needs: asset identity and versions, action/pose mapping, contact and attachment points, station state, camera and dialogue cues. Engine maps those needs to a supported architecture. Reading animation while factory time is held, for example, must remain presentation motion; it cannot silently advance card custody or production. Preserve that required behavior without declaring a new protocol schema here.

## Precedence and evidence

An explicit current owner decision outranks an old prescription. Current code establishes what actually runs, not what the owner must want. Approved current briefs establish intended behavior; unapproved drafts and this packet remain proposals. The archive is evidence of design lineage. Search results and agent memory must preserve those distinctions.

Known stale passages need scoped corrections through Producer: the canonical pre-E1 cast and reference-study conclusion still describe helper checks before STILETTO; the storyboard-composite notes retain that older chronology. E1's current script and the owner's latest sequence take precedence. Keep useful visual compositions while marking obsolete timing notes; do not reapprove or regenerate art merely to erase its history.
