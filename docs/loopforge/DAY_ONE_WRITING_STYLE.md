# Writing style

When jo corrects max’s impression of LIMEN, he objects to one word. max has said *pleasure*. It ought to be *privilege*. A man could take pleasure in seeing jo. In six years at that gate jo has seen no evidence of it. *Privilege* he’ll allow. It’s a word LIMEN can use every morning without ever having to look pleased. This is what six years of working here sounds like in somebody’s mouth.

### What the workers do with the wait

In the *Pulp Fiction* screenplay Jules and Vincent reach the apartment door in the middle of an argument about a foot massage. Vincent checks his watch. They’re early. They step away from the door and Jules picks up the very point on which Vincent had cornered him. We know whose business the appointment is. The argument is theirs. The clock gives them another minute and we learn what Jules would spend it on. [The hallway scene](https://dailyscript.com/scripts/pulp_fiction.html)

The studio opened with “Is this the right queue?” Once bob got an answer, he’d used up his reason to speak. The rewrite found an argument the workers could happily have tomorrow. Give jo his whole complaint. Give max “I haven’t finished.” He wants his audience back. Then bring in LIMEN and let him say *privilege*. He settles the argument without knowing there was one. bob is still watching max when everybody else has turned around. That extra instant of pleasure belongs to the newcomer. The old hands know when the joke’s over.

The same test costs the first rewrite one of its jokes. After the blade catches bob, sam offers his hand for bob to squeeze and complains, “Christ, bob. Leave me the use of a finger.” The trouble starts at the setup. sam has to offer exactly that comfort so he can deliver exactly that punch line. Consider the studio’s plainer “bob. Stay down.” An actor has somebody to say that to and reason to fear he’ll get up. In the approved cut sam goes straight to his knees. ed, close enough to do the same, puts his boot on the skidding card. Let the player see which of the two each man went to catch.

### How long it takes bob to hear something

In the opening of Astafiev’s *Cursed and Killed*, the recruits hear a howl through a wagon wall. Lyoshka shifts his cap off one ear. The men work out that it’s singing and still crane their heads as they’re marched out. Knowing *what* they heard has made them want to know *who*. At the gate a click behind max’s ear catches bob. “How’d you do that?” “Which bit?” max has been working on a voice and a jaw. Either would seem worth asking about to the man who can do both. A line specifying which trick interests bob would deprive max of a very good reason to answer. [Astafiev, Book One, chapter one](https://mybook.ru/author/viktor-astafev/proklyaty-i-ubity-4/read/)

The artist who painted Conveyor put a loaded tray between sam’s two hands. Look at what that asks of his shoulders. Assigning him Wash and Forge, as the studio did, throws away work the painting has already done. The approved “You there. Intake.” lets STILETTO size up a worker about whose name she shows no curiosity. At the jammed door bob does want to know something about her. How can she be so sure a blow will free it? “I’ve stood at this fucking press, bob.” Try stressing *stood*. For the time it takes him to hit the door, we can picture her in his place. She still wants the next brain. It’s the only time she uses a worker’s name, and the actor can decide how much recognition to put into it. [The Conveyor and Security paintings](/stepanoskin/loopforge/design#day-one-intro)

The studio gave bob “One.” That is a line for someone counting stock. “I made that” gives him an instant to look back at his work and gives STILETTO a reason to make him turn around. Save the count for lou. By the time he says “Three,” we’ve followed a brain from sam to bob to jo to max. A number is exactly what the man at Output would have for them. The factory and the workers can keep different accounts of the same accomplishment.

### What the last impression is worth

In the morning-count frame from *Back to the Dawn*, Bruce tells the assembled inmates what the first of the month brings. Look down from his speech panel and the people he’s insulting are still there to be looked at. Give LIMEN that room. A working pass had gus say “I can call the next fellow for you. I carry pretty well.” It asks him to explain a voice we could hear for ourselves. Worse, he’s figured out the job before LIMEN has offered it. Overlap “I DO!” with max’s “you, sir!” and gus can discover what he’s volunteered for when LIMEN points him to the desk. By “NEXT!” the same magnificent voice is running the queue. lou has had time to say “noone...” to exactly one friend. All four replies have to start before any man can reconsider his answer in light of the other three. [The count and the cell, with gameplay frames](/loopforge-design/narrative-reference.html)

Dostoevsky gives Marmeladov a nearly buttonless dress coat when he introduces him in the tavern. The one remaining button is fastened. Someone took the trouble. You can see the condition he’s in and the terms on which he’d still like to meet a stranger. Give ed that much consideration. When he asks whether to start a fresh page, he offers LIMEN a choice of pages and quietly takes the job of keeping the register for himself. At the bell, let him mean every word of “All eight accounted for, sir.” If he plays it for a laugh, max has nothing to steal. [Crime and Punishment, Part I, chapter II](https://etc.usf.edu/lit2go/182/crime-and-punishment/3393/part-1-chapter-2/)

At a family dinner in *Attack on Titan*, Reiner is supposed to be telling his relatives how terrible the islanders were. Years later he can still remember who kept the larger piece of a potato. A comrade would remember just such a thing. Let a shift be worth remembering in that much detail. Outside, max has ed’s report word for word. It’s an impression good enough for gus to request another. In the working ending jo had a line to tell us what max got right. The final cut gives the first laugh to ed, after max does LIMEN. He knows the voice too. He can spend the day wanting a place at that desk and still want to walk home with these men. Tomorrow a writer can put him back at the gate with bob’s card in his pocket and either pleasure available to him.
