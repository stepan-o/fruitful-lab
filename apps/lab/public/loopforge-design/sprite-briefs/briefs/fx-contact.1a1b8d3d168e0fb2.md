# Mechanical contacts & small dust — fx-contact

Provide a restrained physical accent for stamp, tray landing, clamp snap, press release and cortex landing.

## Group

Effects

## Delivery priority

P1

## Quantity

1 reusable effect family

## Parts and source layers

4–6 frame impact accents, light dust puff and short metal glint; each available as a still pose.

## States and variants

One-shot only on a confirmed presentation cue; no idle particles.

## Assembly and sockets

Anchor to corresponding machine or prop contact; scale to contact size. Sound/FX can be suppressed independently.

## Acceptance

No repeated cue when resuming or replay seeking; effect never substitutes for moving the actual door or prop.
