# Pause, resume, restart & loading states — ui-session

A small coherent skin for starting the scene, pausing, muting, readable loading/error states and restarting the rehearsal. Use existing browser/ENGINE widgets where possible.

## Group

Interface

## Delivery priority

P1

## Quantity

1 control family

## Parts and source layers

Play/pause, sound on/off, restart, speed indicator and help icons; scalable button/panel/focus tokens. No separate elaborate menu illustration.

## States and variants

loading/ready/playing/paused/error/retry; mute and reduced-motion preferences persist.

## Assembly and sockets

ENGINE owns controls, fonts, focus, progress and text. No artwork waits on network before becoming legible. Missing art uses a labelled silhouette fallback.

## Acceptance

No autoplay sound; tab hiding pauses presentation work; pause preserves roots, props, dialogue and machine phases.
