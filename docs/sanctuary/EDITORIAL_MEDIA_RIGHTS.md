# Sanctuary Economics — editorial media review

Reviewed 3 October 2026. Research guidance for the owner's publication decision,
not legal clearance. Assumed home jurisdiction: Canada; confirm this before
relying on a jurisdiction-specific conclusion. Vercel's US notice process is also
relevant. No publisher was contacted and no bespoke permission was obtained.

## Scope

An independently authored, free industry-analysis essay on fruitfulab.net.
Images identify and explain games, design systems and commercial interfaces.
They are not game assets for Loopforge, merchandise or another game. This is a
favorable editorial context, but neither attribution nor absence of direct
revenue is a blanket permission to reproduce every asset.

## Three distinct questions

1. Does the exact use fall within a publisher's permission or an asset-specific
   press license? Record the terms and their date; a download button alone is
   not a general republication license.
2. If not, is the exact use covered by a statutory exception? The answer depends
   on its purpose, context and extent, not merely the label "analysis."
3. Could a complaint disrupt hosting even while the legal position is defensible?
   Preserve provenance and a removable media layer.

Canadian criticism/review fair dealing requires attribution of the source and
the named creator, where supplied. Fairness is separately assessed: purpose,
character, amount, alternatives, nature and market effect. An image can be useful
evidence of something words or an invented diagram would not adequately show.
Sources: [Copyright Act s.29.1](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-42/Section-29.1.html?wbdisable=true),
[CCH, 2004 SCC 13](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/2125/1/document.do).

Canada also has a conditional noncommercial user-generated-content exception
for individuals creating a new work. Attribution, a reasonably lawful source
and absence of substantial adverse market effects are relevant conditions.
Do not assume a company publication or business-promotional project qualifies
merely because there is no paywall.
[Copyright Act s.29.21](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-29.21.html?txthl=reasonable+grounds).

US fair use weighs purpose/character, the work's nature, amount/substantiality
and market effect. Noncommercial criticism is favorable, not conclusive.
There is no safe number of screenshots, percentage, resolution or seconds.
The essay's analytical role matters more than adding a filter or frame.
[US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/).

## Publisher findings

**BG3:** use its [specific terms, updated 31 July 2025](https://baldursgate3.game/bg3-fan-content-terms/),
which incorporate [Wizards' policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy).
The latter permits free fan websites using art, subject to conditions including
an unofficial notice and restrictions on separate logos, other people's IP,
music/video and games. Existing marks/notices inside art must remain intact.
Permission is revocable. Larian's terms grant reuse rights in published fan
content to Larian and partners/licensors. Its general policy expressly excludes
BG3, so do not substitute that policy. Broader permission questions go to Wizards.

**Blizzard:** its [video policy](https://www.blizzard.com/en-gb/legal/2068564f-f427-4c1c-8664-c107c90b34d5/blizzard-video-policy)
permits qualifying free community video productions on creators' websites;
promotional trailers outside games are excluded from its content definition.
This does not clearly license every still image or wallpaper in a web essay.
The [legal FAQ](https://www.blizzard.com/en-sg/legal/c1ae32ac-7ff9-4ac3-a03b-fc04b8697010/blizzard-legal-faq)
uses home/personal/noncommercial wording even beneath a fansite question.
Do not treat that ambiguity as blanket public-image permission.

Its [logo guidelines](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/574308ba-d8db-44e9-bc64-76173f84a57e/blizzard-entertainment-logo-and-trademark-guidelines)
tie permission to qualifying activity policies, require credits and prohibit
logo alteration other than resizing, misleading affiliation, combination with
one's own brand and certain objectionable uses. A guideline is not itself a
universal license for every context.

**Other studios:** check each title's publisher/licensor and the actual asset.
A studio's permission does not necessarily cover fan artwork, licensed songs,
actor photography, an article's photography or unrelated third-party material.
The BG3 home page includes a community fan-art gallery: do not attribute those
works to Larian or assume its display gives us permission to reproduce them.

## Working asset decisions

These are editorial recommendations, not judicial conclusions:

| Use | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Owner-made game screenshot explaining a specific system | Strong candidate for a public editorial-use review; caption the visible evidence. |
| Official screenshot with an identified source | Similar analytical candidate; keep creator/publisher credits and context. |
| Key-art pair introducing BG3 and D4 | Review as a bounded comparison of how the games present their promise. Discuss the actual composition; do not imply the covers prove a revenue model. |
| Large repeated wallpaper used for atmosphere | Prefer Sanctuary's original art, or obtain explicit permission for this use. |
| Game/studio name in prose | Identify the subject clearly. Keep Sanctuary's authorship distinct. |
| Standalone logo in a masthead, favicon or mixed brand mark | Avoid without a reviewed permission basis. An identifiable logo within an unchanged source image is a different use. |
| Soundtrack as background music; ripped textures/models | Do not add under a general "editorial" assumption. Obtain the relevant rights or use original/licensed substitutes. |
| High-resolution downloadable asset gallery | Avoid making the essay a substitute for the source library or its licensing market. |

Trademark analysis is distinct from copyright. Confusion about source or
sponsorship is a key concern; merely identifying a work is different from
adopting its mark as our identity. See [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/page/about-trademark-infringement).
Publisher-policy restrictions are conditions of that permission, not a complete
statement of statutory criticism rights. Independent fair-dealing/fair-use
arguments and any applicable contractual obligations require separate review.

Original illustrations also need judgment. Changing style or medium does not
automatically avoid substantial copying. The existing "Original visuals only"
description is a media choice, not a promise of zero infringement.
[Cinar v. Robinson, 2013 SCC 73](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/item/13390/index.do).

## Hosting and practical exposure

Vercel can disable a page or deployment after a qualifying complaint. Its policy
also covers repeat-infringer account termination. Counter-notices carry identity,
jurisdiction and potential litigation consequences; seek legal advice before
submitting one. Avoiding social-video platforms does not avoid this process.
[Vercel DMCA policy](https://vercel.com/legal/dmca-policy).

We cannot estimate a publisher's enforcement probability. Plan for a direct
removal request or host complaint; neither is a court finding of infringement.
If a dispute escalates, legal costs and remedies become material. For launch,
a focused IP-lawyer review of the actual annotated pages is more useful than
asking whether all game screenshots are allowed in the abstract.

The owner authorizes a public editorial edition using authentic visual citations,
with full credits and documented analytical purposes. This supersedes the earlier
original-only delivery rule. It is an editorial decision under a context-dependent
criticism/review rationale, not a claim that all owners granted individual licenses.

remain disclosed in the register rather than invented. No fan-gallery art is added.

### Hard requirements, conditional requirements and house rules

- **Law:** obtain an applicable permission or fit the actual use within an
  exception. For Canadian criticism/review, mention the source and any creator
  named there, and assess fairness separately. Do not confuse sponsorship or
  misrepresent authorship. Copyright and trademark are distinct analyses.
- **Publisher permission conditions:** where relying on a fan/press permission,
  meet its scope, notices, access, logo and other conditions. BG3-specific terms
  incorporate Wizards' policy; Blizzard's mark guidance depends on qualifying
  activity policies. These conditions are not a universal statement of what
  criticism law permits. Existing notices remain visible.
- **Our editorial rules:** cite images at the point they help an argument; use
  our own graphics for atmosphere, synthesis and diagrams. Faithful reconstructions
  and reversible analytical overlays may explain interfaces when clearly labeled.
  Do not export these images into Loopforge or present the register as a media
  licensing/download library. Revisit the basis if monetization or scope changes.

### Public source records — clarified 5 October 2026

The reader's “Visual sources & use” disclosure is a compact index, not a design
commentary. Each item links to its primary source where known and to the full
rights/use record. Keep the owner and any named creator, source and source date,
license or claimed editorial basis, date of that review, modifications, specific
analytical purpose and provenance gaps in the register. A recorded editorial
rationale is distinct from permission from the rights holder. Do not invent a
source URL, authorship or license when the handoff did not identify one.

Credits already displayed beside a figure remain there. Do not copy captions,
describe visible details again or explain the construction of our graphics in
the chapter disclosure. Keep original-art production notes in the internal
visual atlas. Identifiable works referenced by original catalog parodies retain
their source and creator records without the composition/animation explanations.
This revision changes presentation of existing records, not the underlying
publisher permissions, statutory excerpts or asset publication decisions.

### Corrections and removal

The public source register links to the project's issue tracker for corrections
and rights concerns. A substantiated concern can be handled at asset level while
leaving original prose and graphics available. Removing an asset from a future
manifest alone is insufficient to retract an already-public immutable file:
remove affected bytes/old manifests as appropriate, purge CDN caches and address
host notices deliberately. A statutory counter-notice requires a separate legal
decision. Never automatically respond to a complaint with one.
