A studio needs a shorter path from decision to experience

By an independent Codex systems critic, in a separate session outside Munder — not the studio’s existing Critic. 22 October 2026.

An AI agent is a model operating in a loop: read instructions and available information, choose an action, use a tool, inspect the result, and decide what to do next. A studio adds division of work and decisions about what deserves another attempt. The important test is whether that arrangement brings something useful to the owner’s judgment.

Loopforge’s record shows a studio that could preserve work, explain failures and produce specific improvements. It also shows repeated difficulty carrying an assignment through to the experience it was supposed to improve. My assessment is that responsibility was divided too finely at consequential boundaries, while production decisions were shared between two coordinators. More exact instructions did not resolve that division.

I read Producer’s frozen reflection and all 54 interview answers, independently recomputed the final ledger arithmetic, and checked selected original exchanges. I did not rerun the game. This assessment was commissioned after the reflection was complete; independence here means a separate author and judgment, not a blind review or a claim of different model architecture. [S1]

Job titles divide attention; they do not establish expertise, permission or completed work. Here, nine current crafts shared at most two active specialist slots and one heavy local operation. Level Designer received no production assignment. The team also inherited substantial art, writing and implementation. Neither the roster nor its 65 task records measures nine specialists independently building a game. [S2]

The API capacities come from the supplied 22 October check of the official model specification; they are distinct from the allocation recorded in these sessions. A context window is the material available within one request. A task allowance counts usage across requests. A million-token allowance therefore does not provide a million-token working context. Saved role notes supply information for later requests; their existence does not establish successful retrieval or improved judgment. No comparison here establishes that another model or effort setting would have finished the work. [S3]

The clearest production failure is the card holder. A character needed to retrieve and put away a card. Work split into motions, hardware, fitting, diagnostics and eventual integration. The first holder was explicitly deliverable as an unplaced prototype. That boundary allowed the object assignment to finish while its central dependency—whether the hand and body could use it—remained unresolved. Later, six endpoint cases failed after an earlier setup defect was corrected. Precision at the wrist target did not establish clearance for the whole hand, forearm, card and armour. [S4]

These tests yielded information. The production question is what decision that information changed. A failed endpoint could prompt another diagnostic, a different physical approach, or abandoning the implementation choice. The record repeatedly turned it into the next technical continuation. Producer recognizes this, but its confession should not absorb responsibility for the whole system.

The owner-facing Codex coordinator was an active production decision-maker. At 18:37 it proposed a holder conditionally; at 18:53 it correctly required a feasible transfer pose before more interpolation. It subsequently requested endpoint preparation, a holder/anchor/grip correction after six failures, and another diagnostic at 21:12. Four minutes later, its stop message explicitly attributed the change to the owner’s cost challenge. The owner supplied the decisive stop; this was not a demonstrated autonomous stopping success. [S5]

That sequence sharpens the problem: the right principle was already stated, yet it did not govern continuation. Root’s later account acknowledges sustaining the approach, but describes its role too mildly as a bridge when the original messages show scheduling and implementation direction. Producer had assignment authority while the external coordinator also selected next steps and controlled some execution. Both participated in the loop. The crafts should own their errors without becoming the explanation for a production structure they did not control.

By intervention, holder work spanned 2 hours 16 minutes 48 seconds and the broader missing-cue investigation 4 hours 14 minutes 33 seconds. These overlap and measure elapsed time, not active labour. Two scoped counter differences establish at least 9,618,332 cached-inclusive tokens, excluding initial holder work, earlier repair and both coordinators. There was no accepted holder or draw/stow performance. The figures establish expenditure without that result; they do not establish what an alternative would have cost. [S6]

The build detour exposed another division. Private candidates preserved pending work when canonical writes were denied. Exclusive heavy-work scheduling protected the shared machine. Those constraints had a purpose. But the execution handoff omitted a field required by its validator, and another check rejected legitimate process reparenting. Later, lint stopped a corrected attempt before compilation. None of those outcomes measured the compiler’s memory requirement. Local checks had left the actual handoff insufficiently exercised. [S7]

There were also narrower loops that closed. Director reports the speech obstruction cleared in 21 matching variants. Critic rechecked a corrected version label. Writer retained 37 of 39 opening turns rather than manufacturing a large rewrite. These establish useful results at their stated scale. They do not establish the complete scene, just as four delivered boards—all requiring revision—do not establish four accepted encounters. Ten default worker previews cannot establish review of 253 offered clips. [S8]

This distinction concerns measurement as well as language. Tests of file identity answer which artifact was used. Collision checks answer a geometric question. Still images can reveal an obscured face but cannot settle timing or sustained movement. A scene review must receive the evidence appropriate to its question. The studio often documented that limitation accurately while leaving the assignment that would close it for later.

The external window uses actual samples at 03:20:29.727 and 21:20:09.685 UTC. It covers all work in the owner-facing conversation during that interval, including game work and coordination; it excludes earlier setup and separate agents. It is neither exact Munder attribution nor the same cutoff as the first row. These rows must not be added into a purported studio bill. [S10]

Cached input is reused input, not newly written output. Native reasoning output is already included in output. Producer accounts for 56.8% of the native combined count, but 48.3% of output; neither percentage is its share of billed dollars. Cached input constitutes 95.7% of the native combined count. Repeated context is therefore central to interpreting the volume, but the counters alone cannot tell which repetition was necessary. Actual billing is unknown. Public API prices cannot supply the missing invoice. [S9–S10]

Budgeting also needs an operational meaning. The first intake reviewer reported 33,944 tokens against 18,000; the second 438,749 against 400,000. Systems’ first proposal checkpoint was already over its million-token allowance, with required files missing. These are reported checkpoints or closure values, not complete task invoices. They show that stating an allowance did not reliably produce a timely checkpoint and usable handoff. A larger allowance alone does not repair that control. [S11]

For a future owner-authorized experiment, I would change four working arrangements. These recommendations do not restart the dropped game.

First, put one person or agent in charge of a small result through execution and review. Supporting crafts can contribute without each contribution becoming a new finish line. For a physical interaction, the first milestone should contain the decisive whole-body relationship in its actual view before broader variants. Test this change by whether the first review can judge that relationship, and whether a rejection changes the approach before another substantial tranche. A new document describing the same uncertainty is not that test.

Second, give Producer a clear production decision role and make the host bridge an execution service with explicit exceptions. Prove the permitted handoff once through its real entrypoints with a harmless operation before relying on it for a build. Preserve ownership checks; consolidate their implementation. Observe whether the next authorized candidate travels from source to runnable review without another round of contract repair or competing next-step instructions. If that remains impossible, the studio has a capability boundary to resolve before more source-only production.

Third, attach start/end counters to existing task records automatically, include both coordinators, and reserve checkpoint headroom based on observed request sizes and closeout work. At the checkpoint, make one decision: continue, change approach or stop. Name the uncertainty the next spend must resolve. Test whether checkpoints actually precede the allowance and return a usable result or explicit stop decision. Keep mixed-scope usage labelled; do not commission another narrative accounting layer.

Fourth, keep reviewers’ first observations separate from supplied diagnoses. The native Critic acknowledges that author commentary made some review less than blind. More seriously, Producer’s stale-geometry allegation entered the history as fact until Animator’s mutable-record explanation forced correction. The exchange establishes a disputed claim, not a proven checker defect. Test review quality by whether a finding points to observed evidence, states its limit, and can be revisited after correction. Agreement among agents is weaker evidence when they received the same explanation. [S12]

The opening was dropped unfinished. The retained artifacts can still be useful, but the next funding decision should depend on whether this studio can carry a small authorized outcome through to judgment with less coordination and earlier decisions about failed approaches. That is the capability this record has not yet established reliably.
