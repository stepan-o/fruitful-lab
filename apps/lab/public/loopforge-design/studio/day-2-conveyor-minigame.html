Day 2 Conveyor minigame · Handoff to GAME SYSTEMS DESIGNER

GAME SYSTEMS DESIGNER handoff

Loopforge v2 · From WRITER to GAME SYSTEMS DESIGNER · Owner-approved opening · 20 November 2026

You are GAME SYSTEMS DESIGNER for Loopforge. Design the playable Conveyor minigame that follows the Day 2 dialogue. The Producer’s morning appointment gives the same factory two different starting conditions. Show what the Producer can perceive, decide and change on each route. Start with the shared Studio instructions and your maintained role brief.

Read both versions of Day 2

  • LIMEN at Security, STILETTO on Conveyor. Read 2P–2G, including bob’s missing-ID check, the pass decision, the washed carrier held for jo, and STILETTO going to see where her operator is. The printed run uses the pass. Detention can leave Wash unstaffed. Identify the first playable Conveyor decision this route supports and explain how work already in progress and the live Security decision coexist. Day 2 · The photograph
  • STILETTO at Security, LIMEN on Conveyor. Read from the bids through “This time max looks straight at LIMEN and waits.” That wait is the owner’s start for the minigame. At the end of the reading the second carrier is at Forge. Decide what can happen next through play. Day 2 · Posts swapped

Check the existing 2D Shifts prototype, the behavioral specification and the BDI companion. Report which relevant rules run today and which need ENGINE work.

Exact entry on the swapped route

  • All ten workers passed on STILETTO’s shout. STILETTO is pacing Security alone. LIMEN supervises Conveyor. sam and ed are at Intake, bob at Wash, jo at Belt and max at Forge. The Producer has one in-shift intervention available.
  • The first press stroke was empty. LIMEN’s stop, repeated “Twitch,” Protocol lesson, two full seconds of silence and first Forge clearance led to one finished cortex. STILETTO saw that one cortex. Her objection, LIMEN’s answer and her return to Security have played. The second two-second silence fell between “Where was I...” and “Ah yes. The Protocol.”
  • jo has moved the second carrier on LIMEN’s Belt clearance. max has seen the consequence of jumping the call, has his foot off the treadle and is watching LIMEN. The second Forge clearance is pending. Start in this live state with the shift clock, crew positions and first cortex intact.
  • ed holds his own ID and bob’s. During the second Wash, sam heard ed claim he picked up a card. bob heard neither line. sam has no evidence of whose card it is. K04 spent one Conveyor conversation opportunity. The rack and departure exchanges are later possibilities if the right workers meet and acquire the evidence. Keep the seven alternate running replies in the bank marked as proposals.

Deliver for owner review and ENGINE handoff

  1. Give one worked first turn for each appointment. State what the player sees or hears, the first actionable decision, what the supervisors and operators do on their own, and what happens if the player keeps watching. Identify any new control you recommend and its cost against the existing intervention economy.
  2. Specify the repeated cycle from Intake through Wash, Belt, Forge and rack. Cover station readiness, clearance and receipt, staffing, early or empty strokes, holds, recovery, completed output and the bell. Explain how speed, compliance and worker attention give the two supervisors different results on the same equipment. Preserve the approved lines, their addressees, and the two timed silences in the opening.
  3. Give ENGINE paired, reproducible examples. Include a run from the exact swapped entry, a changed player choice from that same state, and a regular-post run where a different Security outcome changes who can work. Show physical output, waiting time, what each worker could witness, and any consequence carried into the next shift. Include a resume at the waiting Forge that preserves the single cortex and the one spent conversation.
  4. Deliver the recommended rules, state and event transitions, acceptance cases and any decisions that still need the owner. The owner is commissioning a design specification. ENGINE integration follows review.
