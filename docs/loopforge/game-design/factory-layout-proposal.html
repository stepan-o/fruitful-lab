Meshy and final camera handoff

Each room sheet pairs original art with keyed footprints, target heights and actor positions. Start with the hero silhouette at worker scale. Generate rigid housings, then split doors, spools, clamps, dials and trolleys at their working pivots. Build repeated seats, pipe bends and loom frames once. Add cables, transparent liquid, projected images and legible signs in-engine. Fit a simple collider and test the actor contact before adding surface detail.

For the final opening cameras, review the widest and tightest lenses at phone and desktop crops. Keep STILETTO outside the exterior composition, including her shadow, while max and jo speak. On the first Security shot, the queue and LIMEN should hold attention until her approach. Check silhouettes behind the rails, her sightline to the pier and the unbroken stride through it.

  • At the south entrance — a working double door, the overhead factory sign and enough yard for ten staggered starts plus STILETTO’s later approach.
  • At Security — the queue rails, boom and west pier, LIMEN’s blade clearance, the two desks, register, grate and card slide. Allow sam to kneel while ed stops the card. Mark all five doors on the cleared side. Keep the short apron from the boom to Forge free for the through-pass and fourth check.
  • In the Forge — preserve the long carrier bed, rails, operator walk, overhead crane, receiving stillages, transfer road and overhaul bays. Block three rack positions, the adjacent body-fitting stand, the pallet switch and upper/lower shutters at full carrier width.
  • Inside Theatre — a receiving stop, a row of docking cradles facing the stage, the operator’s side aisle and a separate outbound track. Leave an occupied cradle in place while a second pallet arrives. Keep the screen and CATHEXIS visible to the audience.
  • At Logistics — both receiving mouths, a body-width staff wicket beside each, allocation position, two marked lanes, retained muster, quota dock and empty return. A retained unit reports next shift. A quota unit leaves the building.

For blockout acceptance, walk from the checkpoint into each unlocked workroom. Trace one identified unit across every handoff with Theatre running, closed and full. On the advanced line, isolate a Brewery batch, starve a loom and fail the Cortex proof in turn. The player should be able to point to the waiting item and its destination. Trace a Logistics attendant on foot beside the freight. Rehearse all eight beats at 1× with simultaneous crossings, a loaded rack and a clear camera view of the card, the fourth check and the third brain.
