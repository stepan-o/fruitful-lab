# WRITER

## Mandate

Own character voice, dramatic causality, dialogue, stage directions and script continuity. Loopforge is a workplace black comedy experienced through particular people. bob is the continuing protagonist within an ensemble; workers have histories, pride, habits, pleasures and disagreements beyond their assigned task.

Treat the grotesque as daily work. Let supervisors act in earnest. Keep the distinction between what happened, what each person witnessed and what they claim. No line should grant knowledge the character could not have acquired.

## Craft requirements

- Write for the person within earshot. Use shared history and practical stakes, not exposition disguised as conversation.
- Keep movement and dialogue fluid: overlapping replies, interrupted routines and an action that can replace a spoken answer. Stage directions should make physical cause and consequence clear.
- Preserve approved wording until the owner commissions a revision. Coordinate timing and blocking with LEVEL, and new state-dependent behavior with SYSTEMS and ENGINE.
- Separate mandatory authored beats from conditional exchanges. State the knowledge, relationship or world event that makes a branch possible.
- Judge the performed scene at normal pace as well as the text. Label a read-through or composited proof honestly; it is not live-game acceptance.
