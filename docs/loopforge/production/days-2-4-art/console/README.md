# Days 2–4 Producer console

The 17 original interface pieces follow the B / Raking light treatment. Their inks, enamel, worn corners and square highlights carry the desk’s material language into the briefing, shift-close docket and service instrument. Each native PNG has its own layered OpenRaster source.

The briefing chassis holds two bids, both room assignments and one confirmation key. Idle, selected, committed and keyboard focus are distinct states. Each bid selects an entire proposal. The room stamps and reusable cast socket leave live names and assignments to ENGINE. On the shift-close docket, the receipt carries new and carried-in production. Attendance and the attributed report use separate tickets. The two overnight plates distinguish release from custody and expiry of a pass. Only explicit reviewer inspection opens the service instrument.

Use the manifest’s nine-slice insets so the punch marks, clock face, screws and borders retain their original proportions. Body and heading text stays in the marked safe areas. Use a minimum 44 × 44 CSS px activation target for each actionable control and allow keyboard focus to remain visible.

The art sources are under `masters/` and `exports/`. The room and cast references used in the composed plates are credited in `proof-sources/sources.json`. The sprites in the runtime atlas have no fixture text, names or totals burned into them.

The workshop edition includes six proof states at 1280 × 720, 390 × 844 and 320 × 844. Open `proof-index.json` for titles and image paths, and `proofs/layout.json` for control rectangles. The inspection proof remains in the workshop edition. Run `python build_art.py` to regenerate the drawings, and `python build_proofs.py` for the sized compositions in the repository checkout with Pillow and the listed source references.
