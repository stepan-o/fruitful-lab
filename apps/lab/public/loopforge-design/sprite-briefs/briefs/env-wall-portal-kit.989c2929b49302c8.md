# Walls, cutaways & doorframes — env-wall-portal-kit

Retain the approved factory outline and room connections. Low cutaway walls describe the building without covering the performance.

## Group

Environment

## Delivery priority

P0

## Quantity

1 modular kit; 14 portal placements

## Parts and source layers

Straight/corner/T/end wall modules, north/east/south/west facings, door jambs and threshold, separate front occlusion pieces; no roof in active rooms.

## States and variants

Open passage and closed/locked door panels; doors to later rooms remain closed. Staff entrance is its own asset.

## Assembly and sockets

Use zones and portals from geometry; do not rescale passages to fit art. Ground collision polygon is independent of painted wall height. Cutaway/opacity responds to camera, not simulation access.

## Acceptance

Arrival and eight-worker exit pass through Lobby–Dispatch–Security without clipping. All 14 portals have a declared open/locked state in ENGINE.
