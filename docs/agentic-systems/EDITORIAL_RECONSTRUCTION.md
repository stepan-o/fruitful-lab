## Argument

Every chapter is a decision about production value versus cost. Production value means a result that serves the commission: a player can perceive and act on a consequence, a system preserves an obligation, or a client can use and maintain the delivery. Cost includes money where observed, compute, elapsed time, human attention, rework, integration complexity and the alternatives forgone. These are not interchangeable quantities. Do not invent a universal score or a price where the record has no invoice.

No standalone cost chapter. No repeated “value / cost” boxes appended to otherwise unchanged technique essays. A concrete situation must expose what is gained, what is consumed, who receives the benefit, and the point at which another increment is unjustified. A useful chapter can recommend spending more. Cheap output and useful production are different outcomes.

The chapter count is a working consequence of distinct decisions, not an acceptance target. Nine is the final reconstructed map. Merge again if the text cannot earn a boundary. Keep strong stories, substantive disagreements, inspectable examples, diagrams, animations, screenshots, source attribution and runnable exercises. Remove duplicate introductions/conclusions, repeated cautions, generic five-paragraph explanations, and recaps of what a figure already makes clear. Some supporting technical detail can remain inspectable beside its example without occupying the main narrative.
