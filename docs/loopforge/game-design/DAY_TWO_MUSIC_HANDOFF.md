## Audio interface

Decode the half-open frame ranges in cues.json against 48 kHz stereo. Convert source frames to seconds before using a resampled audio context. Each room and bed loops between its start and exclusive end. A one-shot's tail lies within its range. The reel has 125 ms guard gaps between cuts. Entries and exits are independently playable. Each bridge spans one bar. For a fresh destination continue at the following bar. The Options→Menu bridge quotes Menu bar 49 and the Conveyor→Security bridge quotes Security bar 17. On a revisit, restore the remembered phrase. Fade the displaced sound over 45 ms and stop it by 50 ms.

Use the existing MusicInputs v1 ordered snapshot. Surface, listening focus, speech, pause and publicly observable running/waiting retain their meanings. Add an optional coarse lineIntensity (two, four, full). Two is the initial default. Day One follows the three staged staffing states. On Day Two+, ENGINE can count visible stations whose projected hold reads Working. This audition proposes 0–1 working stations for two, 2 for four and 3–4 for full. Clearance and missing crew affect that count through the existing mechanic. Zero activity alone does not establish a wait. Waiting and speech take priority. During either interruption remember the latest tier, then restore it at the corresponding beat. Pause freezes musical time. A same-room change preserves position modulo 32 bars. ENGINE can derive the tier on a public-state change and pass just that value. The only required discrete game event for this pass is a confirmed shift bell. Scene markers follow forward crossings of visible scene boundaries. A restored snapshot sets the bed directly. Deduplicate by session, sequence and receipt ID. Cancel pending one-shots on seek, branch and stop. Resume from the held musical position with fresh public state. Keep music mute independent of voices. No private beliefs, supervisor identity, quota estimate or station-by-station feed is needed.

The desk uses manual listening inputs, a shortened route and the existing LIMEN welcome on a separate voice path. The release leaves its first beat free for the game's bell. ENGINE owns the Rust/KVP-0001 simulation, replay, public projection and connection to real speech, effects and clock state. Music emits no simulation commands. A slow decode, missed beat or interrupted tail never holds a game event.
