# Bloom Whispers Quiz Research + Strategy Brief

Status: research draft for review, 2026-05-23  
Scope: content strategy and quiz architecture only. No website implementation.

Quiz title:

```txt
What Flower Message Do You Need Right Now?
```

Core framing:

```txt
Your flower message is: [Flower Name]
```

Do not frame results as "You are [Flower Name]." The flower is a messenger, not a personality label.

## Strategic Recommendation

The quiz should feel like a small editorial flower reading: emotionally specific, source-informed, and useful enough to save. The best result structure is one flower, one clear message, with rare Power Duos only when the quiz score strongly ties two emotional needs.

Recommended final result set: 9 flowers.

- Hellebore / Christmas Rose - Restoration
- Snowdrop - Renewal
- Foxglove - Protection
- Iris - Expression
- Camellia - Expression / quiet excellence
- Love-in-a-Mist - Creativity
- Heliotrope - Devotion
- Bluebell - Devotion / constancy
- Amaranth - Resilience

These avoid the most generic quiz defaults while staying visually rich and understandable for a general audience.

## Rare Power Duo Ideas

Use only when two top segments are within one point and the user's answers show a real emotional blend.

1. Hellebore + Foxglove - Restoration + Protection  
   For the person who is exhausted because their boundaries have been porous. Message: "Rest is easier when your edges are honored."

2. Snowdrop + Amaranth - Renewal + Resilience  
   For the person beginning again after something serious. Message: "Start small. What matters can still endure."

3. Iris + Heliotrope - Expression + Devotion  
   For the person who needs to say the true thing to someone or something they love. Message: "Let your devotion have a voice."

## Result Templates

### Hellebore / Christmas Rose

Primary segment: Restoration  
Core message title: Rest before the thaw.

#### Your flower message:

You do not have to earn rest.

#### Why this flower is speaking to you:

You may have been holding yourself together by being useful, responsive, and available. Hellebore arrives as a winter-blooming message: softness can appear before life looks fully repaired. You do not need a perfect season, a cleared inbox, or a better mood before you are allowed to be restored.

#### What this flower symbolizes:

In Greenaway's Victorian flower dictionary, Christmas Rose is associated with relieving or quieting anxiety. Botanical sources note that hellebores bloom when little else is flowering, often in late winter or very early spring. They are also toxic, which makes them a good Bloom Whispers flower for beauty with boundaries.

#### The Bloom Whispers message:

Let the night be a shelter, not a test. Bloom quietly. Bloom before you feel ready.

#### A gentle reflection:

Where are you acting as if rest must be justified first?

#### A tiny ritual:

Choose one thing you can leave unfinished tonight. Write it on a scrap of paper and place it somewhere closed, like a drawer or book.

### Foxglove

Primary segment: Protection  
Core message title: Protect what is still becoming.

#### Your flower message:

Not every beautiful thing gets access to you.

#### Why this flower is speaking to you:

You may be feeling tender, over-open, or tired of explaining why something does not feel right. Foxglove is tall, luminous, and not harmless. Its message is not suspicion for the sake of fear. It is discernment. The delicate parts of your life deserve protection while they are still forming.

#### What this flower symbolizes:

Greenaway lists Foxglove with insincerity, a sharper Victorian meaning that can be reworked as a warning to notice what is false. Cambridge University Botanic Garden notes that foxglove produces digoxin and that the whole plant is toxic. This makes it a strong flower for boundaries, caution, and self-protection.

#### The Bloom Whispers message:

Beauty is not the same as safety. Let your yes become more exact.

#### A gentle reflection:

Where have you been overriding your own hesitation?

#### A tiny ritual:

Write one sentence beginning with "I do not have to explain..." Keep it somewhere private.

## Quiz Architecture

Recommended scoring:

- Each answer gives +2 to a primary emotional segment.
- Some answers give +1 to a secondary emotional segment or a flower affinity tag.
- Highest segment determines the result family.
- Flower affinity tags break ties inside Expression and Devotion, where two final flowers exist.
- If two segments tie within one point and the pattern is emotionally meaningful, show a rare Power Duo. Otherwise, return one flower.

Default result by segment:

- Restoration -> Hellebore
- Renewal -> Snowdrop
- Protection -> Foxglove
- Expression -> Iris, unless Camellia affinity is higher
- Creativity -> Love-in-a-Mist
- Devotion -> Heliotrope, unless Bluebell affinity is higher
- Resilience -> Amaranth

## Email Capture Strategy

Best option: Option B, with a warm soft gate.

Show a preview immediately, then invite email for the full flower message. This is stronger for conversion than showing everything first, but warmer than hiding the entire result. The preview should prove the quiz is specific before asking for an email.

Your flower message is ready:

Hellebore
