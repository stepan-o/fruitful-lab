## Initial prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create an exceptionally detailed AAA game interface concept for LOOPFORGE, a grim artificial-brain factory run by robots. A PRODUCER'S CCTV CONSOLE, one continuous purpose-built piece of hardware filling a 16:9 screen, not six separate monitors and not a web dashboard. Landscape 2048x1152.
View directly from the seated operator, almost front-on to the screen, looking very slightly down onto the shallow control deck. One coherent camera perspective.
Composition: the upper 72 percent is a very wide, single rectangular smoked-glass surveillance display, thin internal separators divide it into six camera feeds (3 columns by 2 rows). One restrained outer rim shared by all feeds, thin dividers, maximize screen space. First two feeds show atmospheric amber brain conveyor and red security office; remaining four black switched-off glass with faint room-name tapes. Tiny CCTV camera numbers and REC lamps, not big text. Do not draw six separate chunky housings.
Lower 24 percent is one sculpted heavy blackened enamel control deck with expressive slightly absurd factory bureaucracy: on left a chunky ivory-and-black telephone RECEIVER sitting on a red brass cradle with an amber incoming-call indicator and label LEADERSHIP, center a five-position rotary INTERCOM selector with five small empty supervisor portrait sockets and push-to-call control, right a guarded chunky START lever and paper record slot. Controls are physically large, legible and purposeful, few enough to understand. Telephone handle raised slightly by its ringing mechanism, tasteful amber light pools across nearby scratches, not an explosion. Small cash, workers, line condition and delivery odometer windows embedded in the bridge between glass and controls. No diagrams, annotations or dimension lines.
A SMALL LOW PROFILE ROTATING BEACON is natively recessed into the top right shoulder of this same housing, coherent with its metal plane and viewed from above enough to see its circular glass and reflector. Not floating, not hung in front of the screen. Warm white-amber focused brilliant glint at the lens, short localized cone raking nearby worn brass and raised screws with believable source-obstacle-shadow alignment. Most console remains dark.
Loopforge style: dense tactile late-industrial soot, cracked near-black olive enamel, oily gunmetal, rubbed oxidized brass, rubber seals, torn greasy paper labels, patient etched wear, muted cyan electronics, ember amber signals. Serious cinematic material realism with darkly comic engineering, no cartoon outlines, no bright plastic, no clean sci-fi blue holograms. Coherent art direction like an extravagant old industrial security desk, not generic steampunk ornamental gears. Graphic tension from incoming leadership call, everything else waiting. Rich shadow volume, full-screen composition, no people, no hands, no background room, no surrounding empty border. Native actionable controls should look delightful to touch. Avoid gratuitous gauges or meaningless knobs. Exact main labels only: LEADERSHIP, INTERCOM, START. This is a new concept painting, not a screenshot of the previous UI.
