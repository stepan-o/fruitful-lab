# Cyan blade & restrained impact — fx-blade

Make LIMEN’s one strike readable without gore or a screen-filling flash. STILETTO’s metal blades retain sharp silhouette rather than cyan energy.

## Group

Effects

## Delivery priority

P0

## Quantity

1 effect family

## Parts and source layers

Cyan core/edge mask, short sweep strip, 6-frame spark/contact burst; independent additive layer.

## States and variants

Blade idle emission; single sweep/contact/recovery; no continuous strike loop.

## Assembly and sockets

Bind sweep to blade_tip; impact attaches to bob’s injured leg at confirmed contact. No collision or injury decision inside an effect callback. S9 at (125.260, 130.570) m, 0.12 × 1.12 m.

## Acceptance

Hit target and resulting fall remain visible; reduced motion removes sweep/flash but keeps contact pose and aftermath.
