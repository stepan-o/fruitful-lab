## Who can talk during the shift and on the way out?

Security gets one worker conversation when the second distinct authorized worker bound for Conveyor physically crosses the outbound gate. A stamp, fresh call, unauthorized crossing or arrival at a post adds no passage. The active checker and subject are occupied. Two neighbors still standing in the crowd can spend the slot. The walk-out gets one conversation among actual departing neighbors. ed, gus and bob can each discuss the card with any eligible partner. Belief, motive and a chance to talk are required. Each bank entry carries a reason for each side and the evidence a delivered reply can give the listener.

The jobs and neighbor pairs in these three reads are sample outcomes. A changed order can place different workers together. Hearing someone in another part of a room, or crossing a work post with an empty tray, does not make that person a Conveyor chat partner. bob always seeks a conversation and gets priority among equally safe, feasible opportunities until he has a reply. An unsuccessful attempt can carry him to the next opportunity. The single exit slot can be the last opportunity of his day. A worker alone or held until the others have gone cannot obtain a witness by wanting one.
