## Art review notes

- A lights both open palms and the empty enamel. The reflected streak beside the waiting workers is brighter than the underlying floor reference.
- B separates one small upward gesture against shadow. Its amber highlights on the brass and aisle have the strongest sheen of the five.
- C makes the most graphic separation between the machines. bob’s lowered hand enters the bottom-left margin, so keep type toward the center or right.
- D gives the checking pause a soft, matte finish. The status cabinet has been simplified and ed’s angular skull is less pronounced.
- E makes the halted entrance immediately visible against the backlight. The right-arm cuff reads as a cylinder with the blade attached. The fingers of that hand are indistinct. Preserve the full cuff and show its hand more clearly if E wins.
