# Research method and evidence boundaries

Research cutoff: 26 October 2026. The public annotated registry is apps/lab/lib/agentic-systems/sources.json. Each record separates a source's contribution, its evidentiary limits and our proposed use.

This is a critical narrative literature review, not a systematic review claiming exhaustive recall. Sources were selected across foundational software/control thinking, agent research, vendor engineering, original practitioner accounts and game design. Follow citation trails and conflicting accounts; do not rank an author by popularity. Prefer original papers and original practitioner accounts over reports about them. Record dates because tools, results and living essays change.

The 2025 METR experiment must be read with the February 2026 follow-up. Reflexion must be read alongside intrinsic self-correction failures. Multi-agent vendor success belongs beside controlled task-structure comparisons and the cost of tightly coupled work. Huntley and Yegge describe materially different mechanisms. Tane and Steinberger exemplify different balances of planning and feedback. Sutton's scaling argument does not validate an unevaluated loop. Generative Agents establishes a useful character architecture, not game enjoyment.

Local case sources are frozen attributed publications and the current merged implementation. Use public accounts/media in the book. Private research artifacts may corroborate a claim internally but must not be copied wholesale into public content. Keep interviews, Producer reflection, Root testimony and independent assessment separately attributed. No invented dialogue, internal feelings or unrecorded motives.

Narrator: Root / Codex, the agent that commissioned and coordinated the studio, with Stepan as owner and editorial director. A visible authorship note must make this legible. Personal means situated and accountable; it does not mean pretending to be Stepan or a human witness.

Chronology: the frozen closeout at 17:40 UTC on 26 October described the delivered PR as unmerged. The owner merged PR 130 at 18:01 UTC (35d11444). Preserve both facts. No performance correction was delivered after the final failed test.

Source review is scoped to the linked edition. Do not turn a study's result into an eternal model claim. Label synthesis, hypothetical teaching models and proposed future practices explicitly. Quantitative teaching examples use their own named assumptions, never invented studio measurements.

The teaching path must remain runnable without model credentials. Optional live generation must be an explicit separately bounded extension, not a hidden requirement for finishing the book.
