The direction makes the equipment feel like heavy electrical plant machinery: cast black-green iron, broad protective outriggers, a top rubber carrying bridge, cream porcelain insulation and protected oxidized copper contacts. Deep monitor mounting feet and U-shaped porcelain-padded supervisor cradles give this alternative a distinct silhouette. The commit actuator is a blank black rubber square inside a cream collar.

The camera is OFF: empty dark glass and a small upper-left reflection. The ragged tape reads **LATTICE FORGE**. LIMEN retains the cracked mechanical skull and cyan eyes; STILETTO retains the closed angular helmet and red T visor. Both speech balloons and the actuator face are blank for later native text. Four matching material icons cover funds, robot workforce, conveyor condition and weekly delivery.

## Proposed palette and type

Authored starting points, not pixel samples: cast black-green iron #111B17; black rubber #111311; cream porcelain #DCD3B8; aged paper #B5A78C; oxidized copper #8C5B37; dark verdigris #3C665D. Reserve cyan #83DCE9 for LIMEN eyes and red #FA421D for STILETTO's visor.

Suggested type: a licensed DIN-style condensed grotesk for short equipment labels, IBM Plex Sans for native dialogue, and IBM Plex Mono or the existing project monospace for values. Preserve the rough handwritten tape label as art.

## Review notes

The final board was visually inspected at native dimensions. Common upper-left lighting, clear operational silhouettes, faithful character identities, blank writing areas and an OFF display were verified. The refinement removed invented branding and slogans, reduced the palette to six material chips and improved porcelain glaze.

Thick rails and insulators consume more perimeter space than the baseline and need restraint in a six-camera implementation. Some metal wear remains stylized branching scratches. The copper close-up remains a block-shaped specimen although the refinement requested a flat contact blade; use the hero's protected contact geometry as the manufacturing reference. Paper grain needs a runtime contrast check. This is a concept direction, not an engineering drawing, functional interface, published asset pack or owner acceptance.
