# End-of-shift bell — audio-bell

A distinctive hard factory bell. It must cut through the machinery, leaving room for the shout.

## Group

Sound

## Delivery priority

P1

## Quantity

1 strike + decay tail

## Parts and source layers

48 kHz 24-bit WAV masters, clean mono localized cues; stereo ambience only. Separate tails and seamless loops. No baked music/voice/environment mix.

## States and variants

One-shot/loop/tail files and a cue sheet; no clipping; preserve headroom for 4 overlapping replies if voices are added.

## Assembly and sockets

Single bell cue before departures; decay may overlap speech but is ducked below it.

## Acceptance

Captioned bell and visible physical action carry meaning when muted. Preview all simultaneous cues together, not only isolated samples.
