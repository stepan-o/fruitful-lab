The four panels establish the first morning. The separate found-card frame shows what happens while bob is down. The scene below follows the same people through the intervals between those images. CATHEXIS arrives later.

1. The morning walk

The factory door is open. Warm light reaches across the wet paving; inside, an empty belt runs over a bright roller. The workers approach in pairs and loose company, their feet finding the drier patches without much success. We follow them towards the entrance, seeing their backs.

bob cranes past sam’s shoulder.

bob: Have they changed that roller?

sam: Just painted it.

bob: No, listen. It isn't squeaking.

jo: Give it a shift.

bob keeps listening. lou bends towards max to finish something they started further down the lane. ben lets them go ahead. kit reaches the door with his pipe still in his mouth.

2. LIMEN’s greeting

Inside the Lobby the group bunches where the passage narrows. ed leaves himself a view of the inspection desk. Through Security, bob can still see the roller.

LIMEN fills the doorway, facing the workers. His free hand comes up.

LIMEN: STOP AND WAIT HERE!!

The nearest workers stop first. Those behind take another step, find a shoulder or a heel in the way, and shuffle back. lou straightens their long neck. bob loses sight of the belt.

LIMEN: Behind the line. The doorway stays clear.

ed checks his feet. kit checks ed’s.

LIMEN: Your ID. Your assignment. One at a time, when I call you.

gus: We're all on Conveyor.

LIMEN: Then this should be very simple.

max turns towards jo and quietly tries the weight LIMEN gave the last two words.

max: Very simple.

jo keeps looking ahead. LIMEN lets the silence last.

LIMEN: WHO REALLY CARES ABOUT THIS FACTORY?!

ed raises a hand at once.

ed: Me, sir.

A broad hand goes up beside him.

gus: I can help.

LIMEN: Good. I need two at Security. We'll start with you.

He indicates the desk. ed makes sure he has been pointed at. bob, nearer the threshold, has his card ready and thinks the waiting is over.

3. bob takes a step

bob: Here, sir.

He steps into the opening. LIMEN’s cyan blade sweeps across his approach.

LIMEN: HEY YOU I SAID STOP!!!!

One electrical contact takes bob’s legs from under him. His card skids across the Lobby floor. sam comes around his feet and kneels beside him.

bob: I had it ready—

LIMEN: I wasn't speaking to you.

sam’s hand settles on bob’s shoulder. bob stays down.

sam: Stay there. Let me see.

LIMEN: EVERYONE PREPARE YOUR IDs.

Cards emerge around the room. sam needs both hands and leaves theirs put away. Hold the view long enough to see what the others do with the instruction.

ed notices bob’s card. He stows his own, bends to collect it and slips it into his pouch. Then he brings his own card out again. He has already volunteered for Security; the found credential belongs with the things he now considers his responsibility. bob is occupied with sam and does not see the pickup. The player can.

ben gives sam room. kit watches LIMEN’s blade. max has stopped looking for jo’s attention.

The first two checks

LIMEN points directly at ed.

LIMEN: You. Come to the desk.

ed waits for the hand to finish moving before crossing.

LIMEN: Name.

ed: ed.

LIMEN: Assigned station.

ed: Conveyor. Unless you need me here.

LIMEN compares the card with the form. ed stands still throughout.

LIMEN: I do. Name, number, time admitted. Leave room for remarks.

ed: How much room?

LIMEN looks over the waiting workers.

LIMEN: Let's see how they behave.

ed puts his card away and takes the register station. bob’s card stays in the pouch with it.

LIMEN: You next.

gus comes forward. His card is almost lost inside his large hand. He holds it out for the check.

LIMEN: Cards in this tray. Cleared cards in that one.

gus: And I move them across?

LIMEN: When I say. Keep them where I can see them.

gus puts his own card away and takes the tray station. Two workers have now been checked and assigned to Security. sam remains beside bob.

4. STILETTO comes through

The main door opens behind the waiting workers. STILETTO is already walking when it reaches its stop.

lou notices first and draws out of the passage. ben moves back. max steps around jo; kit and mae make room on the other side. The gap opens unevenly, from one person to the next. sam and bob are outside the route, low against its edge.

STILETTO: The line is empty, LIMEN.

LIMEN: I haven't cleared—

She reaches the threshold. LIMEN jumps back. His blade comes up, then drops as she passes. STILETTO does not slow down.

STILETTO: Then hurry up.

sam, to bob: Stay down a moment.

She passes the two occupied Security stations on her way to Conveyor. ed follows her with his head, then returns to the register. gus keeps his place by the trays. Neither knows whether to look at LIMEN.

The workers look from the doorway to the place LIMEN has vacated. He returns to it.

Getting up

bob brings his sound leg underneath him. sam stays low through the start of the movement, then releases his shoulder and rises alongside him. bob tests the other foot before trusting it.

The card stays in ed’s pouch. Nobody hands it back. bob is occupied with standing and does not notice its absence.

bob: He didn't ask for hers.

sam: Easy. Let it take the weight.

They take a short, uneven step back into the waiting group. Only now does sam bring out their own card.

bob’s turn

LIMEN: You. Come here.

bob: Now?

LIMEN: Now.

bob limps forward. LIMEN waits for him to settle.

LIMEN: Look at me, bob.

bob: I thought you meant me.

LIMEN: You thought. You didn't wait to find out.

bob stops explaining. LIMEN lowers his voice into the tone he uses for something that ought to be understood.

LIMEN: You know I hit you for the good of the factory.

bob: yeah, i know…

LIMEN waits a moment, then nods.

LIMEN: Good. Conveyor. Go through.

He has forgotten to ask for the card. ed can record the admission, but neither ed nor gus checks it for him. ed keeps the card.

bob looks back at sam, who is still waiting.

bob: I'll wait by the belt.

sam: Go on. I'll find you.

bob turns towards the inner doorway. He reaches Conveyor unaware that his card is missing. Seven workers are still waiting.

LIMEN: Next.

The missing card

Later, bob discovers the empty holder himself and begins looking for the card. He asks workers what they saw and follows the information he can obtain. He has a reason to want his property back even when nobody is checking it.

The next time LIMEN runs Security, he stops bob again. If the card is still missing, his questioning brings the player into the encounter. If bob has recovered it, the encounter acknowledges that fact. This follows LIMEN’s next Security assignment, not a fixed day-two trigger.

STILETTO sends the workers straight through to Conveyor. bob’s search continues without the immediate clearance obstacle. Her indifference does not identify the holder or settle what happened between them.

The first seed is ed. Holder and motive are chosen together when the run begins and remain consistent with subsequent actions. A witness can be mistaken; the actual custody history cannot change to preserve a mystery. bob’s reply to LIMEN ends the conversation. It does not award forgiveness, trust or agreement with the explanation.

Direction for the rehearsal

Keep four compositions: the approach from behind; the greeting across the threshold; a closer view beside bob with enough height to read the fallen legs; and a wider passage view containing STILETTO’s approach, the occupied Security stations and bob on the floor. Return to the greeting view for his admission. Speech bubbles do not trigger cuts. The strike, fall and witnesses stay together; STILETTO’s approach and passage remain continuous.

Workers gather in uneven groups. A gesture begins, finds a listener or object and finishes. Quiet waiting belongs in the scene. Most reactions need no bubble. max’s imitation is small enough that jo can decline it simply by continuing to look ahead.

STILETTO’s Quick Walk and the workers’ withdrawal form one event. LIMEN’s Step Back finishes before she reaches his former position. sam and the grounded bob stay outside both blades’ path. Recovery follows her passage. Review these relations in motion, including the moments between saved poses.

The engine owns the two completed checks, the injury, physical card custody and the assignments. bob’s admission is explicitly unchecked after the reprimand. Perception determines who learns about an action; watching the pickup from the player’s camera does not inform bob. ed and gus’s duties remove two workers from Conveyor. sam remains assigned there.

The local rehearsal now includes ed’s pickup and stow, sam’s fitted kneel, shoulder support and rise, and bob’s unchecked admission. These are authored performances using the encounter engine. The later search and second checkpoint have tested state rules; their complete staged conversations and player intervention remain to be built. Source clips, contact evidence and remaining motion work belong in the production record. This script is the direction for the scene, not a claim that every glance or handling gesture has passed final visual review.
