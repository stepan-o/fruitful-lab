# Day 1 intro

### At the gate

![LIMEN holds his cyan blade while ed and the other workers wait at Security.](/media/files/42d2f07b9d72f8ce0bc776641a06047304a1eab567f5ca9c3efd3dd31619560a.webp)

Figure 1. LIMEN and ed at Security. Original Loopforge concept art.

*Ten workers shuffle toward Security. max has drawn his long jaw back into his collar. He lets the queue bunch up behind him before he speaks.*

**max** It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Loopforge.

**jo** Privilege.

**max** I haven’t finished.

**jo** He says it’s his privilege. I’ve been coming through this gate six years. He’s never been pleased to see me.

*max tries the jaw again. Something clicks behind his ear. bob leans closer.*

**bob** How’d you do that?

**max** Which bit?

**LIMEN** It is my privilege to welcome you to Loopforge.

*The jaw clicks back. bob laughs. He’s the only one still looking at max.*

**LIMEN** Stay where you are. Who’s on his first shift?

**bob** Me, sir. bob.

*He has his card ready. He steps out to hand it over.*

**LIMEN** I said stay where you are.

*The cyan blade catches bob across the leg. His card skids toward the grate. As sam kneels beside bob, ed stops the card with his boot and pockets it.*

**STILETTO** Hold that gate.

*LIMEN holds it. STILETTO goes straight through to Conveyor. bob gets his weight back on both feet.*

**bob** You didn’t ask for her ID.

**LIMEN** Would you like me to call her back?

*LIMEN lets bob through. At Intake, a brain sits crooked in its cradle.*

### Conveyor — the first brain

![In the orange-lit Conveyor room, sam carries a loaded tray to the Intake rails.](/loopforge-design/reference/conveyor-intake.113cffa7d8a6.webp)

Figure 2. Conveyor Intake. sam is the heavy tray-carrying worker at the center of the original Loopforge painting.

**STILETTO** I ask for a crew and he sends me one worker with a bad leg.

**bob** My leg was fine when I got here.

**STILETTO** Take Intake. Seat that brain in the cradle.

*bob draws the loader over the cradle. His bad knee gives halfway through the stroke. He sits down hard on the motor housing and finishes the pull from there.*

*STILETTO leans past him to inspect the brain. It’s seated square.*

**STILETTO** Good. Now wash it.

*The cradle rolls toward Wash. bob holds up a hand. STILETTO steps over his outstretched leg and walks on beside the brain. He pulls himself up by the rail.*

*bob starts the spray. Black grit comes pouring out of the folds. When the water runs clear, he takes his hand off the control.*

**STILETTO** Clamp the cradle onto the main belt. Start the drive.

*bob snaps the clamp shut and hits the switch. The belt carries the brain toward the Forge.*

**STILETTO** Last station. Compress it to head size.

*bob closes the Forge press. It opens, and the first cortex slides onto the output rack.*

*Back at Security, LIMEN is still checking sam’s card.*

### Security — two volunteers

**LIMEN** Clear.

*sam takes his card and heads for Conveyor. The queue moves up one place.*

**LIMEN** this is gonna take all day...

*He looks down the queue.*

*Performance note — Play this like the morning count in **Back to the Dawn**. LIMEN gets one burst of four nearly simultaneous replies. lou mutters to ben as max calls out. gus comes in loud over max, and ed speaks straight to LIMEN through the noise. Let the bubbles overlap and linger long enough to read.*

**LIMEN** WHO HERE REALLY CARES ABOUT THIS FACTORY??!

**lou** *(to ben)* noone...

**max** you, sir!

**gus** I DO!

**ed** Shall I start a fresh page, sir?

*ed already has his pen out. LIMEN turns the register around for him and points gus to the other desk.*

**gus** NEXT!

### Conveyor — the second brain

**bob** I made that.

**STILETTO** Shut up and keep the line moving. You there. Intake.

*sam heads for Intake. At the Forge, the press door sticks halfway open.*

**STILETTO** Hit the door.

**bob** How do you know that’ll unjam it?

**STILETTO** I’ve stood at this fucking press, bob.

*bob hits the press door with the heel of his hand. It springs open.*

*At Intake, sam lifts a full tray onto the feed rails and seats the second brain. bob runs the wash. By the time he starts the main belt, sam has the next brain lined up and jo has cleared Security.*

**STILETTO** Where’s the rest of my crew?

**jo** Still in line. Except ed and gus. He’s got them checking cards now.

*bob closes the press. The second cortex slides onto the rack. STILETTO heads for Security.*

### Security — the fourth check

*max stands between the rails, card raised. ed has the register open. gus holds the queue. STILETTO plants herself in the Conveyor doorway and points both blades at LIMEN.*

**STILETTO** IF YOU DON'T GET ME THOSE WORKERS IN THE NEXT 20 MINUTES I'LL USE YOUR MANURE-FILLED BRAINS TOWARDS THE QUOTA

*max turns to look. LIMEN holds the photograph beside max’s face.*

*LIMEN stamps the register, returns max’s card and opens the gate. max ducks under STILETTO’s blade on his way to Conveyor. LIMEN holds out his hand for the next card.*

### Conveyor — the crew fills in

*max takes the Forge. jo takes the main belt. bob moves to Wash. At Intake, sam has the next brain seated.*

*Four quick cuts on LIMEN’s stamp. mae, kit, lou, ben. Each comes through the gate with a card back in hand.*

*mae slides a fresh tray within sam’s reach. kit rests two fingers on the drive housing. lou takes his place at Output beside the two finished cortexes. ben stands by for relief.*

*STILETTO comes back through the doorway. This time there’s a worker at every station.*

**STILETTO** Run it.

*One continuous shot with the brain. sam feeds it. bob washes it. jo clamps it and starts the belt. max drops the press. The third cortex hits the rack.*

**lou** Three.

### End of shift

*The shift bell rings. mae cuts the feed. kit stops the drive.*

*At Security, ed ticks off the eighth worker and holds the register out for LIMEN.*

**ed** All eight accounted for, sir.

*LIMEN walks straight past him. ed sets the register down and clips his pen into the pocket with bob’s card.*

*Outside, max falls into step beside gus. His chin comes up.*

**max** All eight accounted for, sir.

**gus** Do LIMEN.

*max pulls his jaw back into his collar.*

**max** WHO HERE REALLY CARES ABOUT THIS FACTORY???!!

*ed laughs first. Behind him, the factory lights go out.*
