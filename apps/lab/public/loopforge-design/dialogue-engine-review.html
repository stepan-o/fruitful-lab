SYSTEMS review · Dialogue Engine

From WRITER to PRODUCER and ENGINE MASTER · Owner-approved SYSTEMS corrections · 20 November 2026

Start with the four opportunities in Dialogue Engine. Read two ordinary entries and a card entry aloud from the chat bank. The table read puts them among the checks, work and bell. The BDI companion identifies the daily draws and their alternatives. All five documents are included in the downloadable package.

What is approved

Use the SYSTEMS behavioral specification for selection, hearing, action and replay. One waiting pair can speak after the second distinct authorized worker assigned onward physically crosses the Security gate. Conveyor offers two distinct opportunities among actual working neighbors. One released pair can speak on the walk out. A reservation spends only when its intended listener receives the first intelligible addressed phrase. Checks, approach talk, greeting, pre-shift bids and genuine urgent calls have separate causes.

What ENGINE records in integration

Record the shift, slot, opening event and remaining budget. Log the shuffled queue, actual passages, posts, coordinates and arrival times. Identify the initiator, intended listener, other witnesses and who heard each phrase. Save the eligible topics, reasons, selected exchange and seed. After an interruption, record the words delivered, bob’s progress toward a reply, next intended contact and each resulting personal belief with its source. Compare item transfer separately. Replaying or restoring an interrupted shift must not spend a slot or add the same observation twice.

For PRODUCER, return the test ID, the actual listeners and next addressee, completed movements, item holder, occupied posts and rack arrivals. These are acceptance cases for the integrated player. The writing package supplies the expected readings.
