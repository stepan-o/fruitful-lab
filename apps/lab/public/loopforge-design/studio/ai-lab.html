What reaches review?

Choose an assignment to see its deliverable and the evidence that goes with it.

Art & 3D assets
ART DIRECTOR + artists

An approved sketch becomes a Meshy candidate, an editable master and a versioned game export.

Owner reviews

A 2D contact sheet or 3D turntable, scale, materials and in-engine silhouette. Match the selected asset to its task ID, rights and credits.

Writing & case studies
WRITER + PRODUCER

Dialogue variants, a sourced case study or a script revision alongside the approved manuscript.

Owner reviews

The exact passage changed, the factual source or build it describes, and the correction that led to the final wording.

Systems & interface design
SYSTEMS + UI DESIGNER

A behavioral contract, wireframe or clickable prototype showing the proposed interaction.

Owner reviews

A rule and its counterexample, a phone and desktop layout, and the interaction at its intended size.

Code & playable prototypes
ENGINE + UI

A scoped commit, tests, PR and playable preview. Rust owns simulation changes. Both skins consume the same public state.

Owner reviews

Native and Wasm checks, footage tied to the build, and a playthrough from the ordinary game entry.

Browser QA
QA + PRODUCER

A browser runner exercises both supervisor routes against one pinned preview, with assertions and recordings.

Owner reviews

The failed step or completed journey, replay ID, device and build. Timing, voice and feel still need a player’s judgment.

Music & voice
MUSIC DIRECTOR + voice production

An approved cue or processed line with the source take, rights record and playback preview.

Owner reviews

Matched listening clips in the scene, the chosen take and its processing history.

What would earn a playable trial?
Isolate the change
Compare authored rules and line, proposed line with recipient fixed, and proposed recipient with line fixed. Combine them only after each shows a benefit.
Challenge the evaluator
Reject an H2 claim that mentions Ed’s desk or any claim of ownership. Accept a faithful paraphrase. Try unavailable listeners, competing actors, duplicates and rewind.
Review the evidence
Blind-score voice, grounding, repetition and timing on held-out scenes. Ask fresh players why Bob chose his listener. Require native/Wasm agreement and zero clock stalls.
