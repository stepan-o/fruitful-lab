# Agent teams in creative production — 27 October 2026

This revision asks when an agent team earns the effort of organizing it. The subject is AI-driven production systems; Loopforge supplies a bounded creative-production case. The studio's sequence is retained only where it explains a dependency, changed judgment, delay or stopping decision. The account does not establish an equal-budget single-agent counterfactual or a universal verdict on swarms.

The team consists of separate working contexts, shared versioned records, delegated work, review and owner authority. “Team” does not imply a collective mind. Creative quality, finished output, reusable capability, total resources and time to delivery remain different returns and costs. Root's coordination is examined separately from the native studio's measured usage.
