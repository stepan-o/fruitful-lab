# Chapter 5 writing review

**Verdict: accept the writing. No material issue requires revision before the separate visual review.**

Reviewed `apps/lab/lib/agentic-systems/revised-chapters/05.json`, adjacent Chapters 4 and 6, its source records, the retained Visual Artist/Scene Director/Animator interviews, and the primary MDA and CrafTeam texts. No repository files were edited.

## Why it passes

- The chapter owns a distinct question: what must creative direction protect when individually correct contributions meet? The balloon, inspection choice, body performance and shared contact each develop a different part of that question. Chapter 4's feedback argument is occasionally recalled, but the new work here is diagnosing the intended experience and the relationships carrying it.
- The voice is specific and human. “The game has helpfully hidden the face doing the shouting” makes the first failure memorable; the author's admission about the inspection rule supplies a real reasoning story rather than a generic lesson. Technical material remains attached to a consequence for the audience.
- Loopforge stays a bounded case. The balloon correction and release-before-rise handoff provide scoped successes; the departure and fitting attempts retain their limitations. There is no invented single-agent baseline or causal claim about team productivity.
- MDA is doing useful explanatory work: the chapter traces a mechanism through the behavior it encourages to the intended experience it defeats. The authored teaching scene is explicitly distinguished from observed Loopforge production.
- CrafTeam is accurately framed as a probe with twelve practitioners, not a controlled productivity result. Its reported move toward direct orchestration is supported by the [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13865v1), especially its abstract and introduction. The chapter's MDA account is also consistent with the [primary framework paper](https://www.cs.northwestern.edu/~hunicke/MDA.pdf).
- The transition to Chapter 6 is earned: preserving meaning in an accepted contribution leads naturally to selecting among generated alternatives. Chapter 5 does not pre-empt Chapter 6's distinct feasibility, ranking and search argument.

Keep the experimental condition in the caption and let the prose begin with the interpretive contribution: the identical hand target can belong to a hesitant novice or a practiced operator.
