# H2 — stepped hand opening and external mounting pads

**H2 is an unfitted hardware/interface proposal. All six H1 endpoint failures remain rejected.** It supplies one geometry revision and an evidence-based Sam-only edge-grip option for Animator to calibrate. No motion or socket was exported or integrated.

## What the saved vertices establish

The 26 entries actually present in `SAM-H2-I1` all match their pins. The dispatch calls this a 27-file packet; no missing entry was invented. Approved Sam image was directly inspected again: broad aged blue armour with dark mechanical joints and brass details, no existing accessible pocket established by that picture.

The original six portrait cases report self275–304, full-right-chain/shell103–105, holder/body105–181,18–30 vertices inside shell,294 unresolved mixed-boundary contacts per case, and zero backing-support samples. Case files are failed prospective endpoints, not an approved pose. Joint labels alone do not prove anatomical region.

`saved-coordinate-evidence.json` converts only the saved triangle vertices into their recorded H1 holder frames by the inverse recorded matrix. No body buffers, skinning or animation were read/evaluated. The72 stored mouth examples (duplicates retained) occupy:

- X -58.454..-25.494mm, Y -35.732..-16.468mm, Z +8.952..+10.523mm.
- The broader saved right-chain triangle subset spans X -92.474..+49.267mm, Y -183.095..+21.522mm, Z -14.495..+198.418mm. These are incomplete evidence bounds, **not the full hand or forearm envelope**.

The mouth examples sit in H1's front plate at Z6.5..11.5mm. Moving that lip solves a demonstrated local obstruction. The wider subset proves that simply widening the13mm slot cannot certify the preserved palm grip.

## External mounting remains a separate fit problem

A rigid bridge connects the lower shell to two rear pads. The nominal mounting plane is33.5mm behind the lower backing and15mm behind the upper backing. These are local construction distances, not offsets from Sam's measured surface. Only the two pad rear faces are proposed intentional body contact; the bridge and all other shell faces must remain outside armour.

Animator must solve a new supported anchor from actual armour and full swept contacts. Require support on **both** pads, keeping the existing conservative3samples/2mm/1cm² screen per pad, then assess surface conformity. Zero backing samples cannot be cured by declaring the old anchor attached or whitelisting its collisions. The local proposed pad faces are triangles178/179 and190/191; all other holder/body crossings remain unintended. Mixed-boundary contacts stay blocking.

## Checks and remaining gates

`build_h2.py` generates both GLB and analytic boxes from the same `make_boxes(parameters)` function. `check_h2.py` independently reads the tiny GLB and checks triangle bounds **and** a shared box-face plane at the original1e-6m tolerance. The strict volume inset stays1e-7m. Positive/negative fixtures cover same-plane/out-of-box triangles, interior triangles, inside/outside tolerance, genuine wall points and the reopened well. Normals, winding, finite data, buffer/index ranges, counts, bounds and byte-identical rebuild pass. The analytic card-only straight sweep avoids the solids, with intended bottom-stop contact.

No full saved surface-intersection recount, body decode, actual-loader run, new endpoint, grip reach/contact, insertion path, support, occlusion or render was performed. These checks must not replace source/runtime collision tests. Preserve Draw2.00s/Stow1.60s,3.6s durations, one-card custody, help/recovery and27unrelated clips.
