The lever could reset and move without the hand following it. Source inspection made the cause explicit: a keyed lever with an unkeyed, unchanged arm path. The repair remounts both and uses matching start, midpoint and end beats. I captured a repeat midpoint with the hand at the knob and inspected complete paused end states. This resolves the specific repeat-state defect within the inspected scope; it is not a claim of frame-by-frame proof throughout the interpolated arc.
2. **Physical construction, gate 6.** Against Sanctuary's actual arcade room and business-chain miniatures, the first workshop read as flat symbols on a background. The relationship deserved a built machine and a grounded control. The revised side volumes, recessed press, window depth, floor perspective and workbench make the assembly a place of inspection while preserving its readable wire break. I re-viewed the revision at desktop, 768, 390 and 320. Additional ornament was not requested or used as the acceptance criterion.

I verified that line at 320, 390 and 768, including keyboard record changes. Small card labels remain pictorial detail; the HTML carries the necessary facts.

The replacement distinguishes setup failure from specimen failure and explains the risk of repairing the wrong thing. The revised live reader preserves this chapter's test-specific contribution.
