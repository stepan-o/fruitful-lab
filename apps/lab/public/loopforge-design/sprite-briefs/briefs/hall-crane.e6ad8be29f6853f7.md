Preserve the proposed Forge hall arrangement and industrial function. This kit establishes the working factory around the active five-station line.

## Group

## Delivery priority

## Quantity

## Parts and source layers

Two runways, moving bridge and hoist on separate layers, ten repeatable columns from geometry.

## States and variants

Static parked state for Day 1. Crane/hoist parts are separable for future movement, but no crane operation or vehicles are required by the approved sequence.

## Assembly and sockets

49×20 m envelope from forgeHall. Ground geometry remains exact; elevated elements use occlusion masks and may fade above active workers. H9 transfer-road striping belongs to env-floor-kit. Contacts are parking/attachment anchors, not new gameplay slots.

## Acceptance

The hall overview matches its build marker; dressing does not occupy roads, staff walk or reserved spare bay. No active animation competes with the current story beat.
