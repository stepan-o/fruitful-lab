Keep max’s foot and the load legible together.

In the desktop replay, the entire line stays visible. Max and the narrow treadle occupy a small part of the machine. In the phone detail they gain space, but the press front, max’s label and the treadle cluster around his feet. This is a visual reading of the frames. The proposed close-up clears that contact point and gives the die a visible travel gap.

2

Give the speaker a place when the lens crops him out.

On desktop the production speech plate sits above LIMEN. Selecting Forge on phone crops his body, and the conversation moves to the left edge. The new glass keeps a room-audio strip with his likeness, name and exact line. Its source is the Conveyor microphone. It carries the room’s audible speech into the close-up.

3

Show the difference between choosing Forge and clearing it.

The integrated interface already has distinct Inspect and Clear controls. The narrow camera can focus a station. The new design gives the desktop the same choice of framing, puts Clear beside its operating point, and retains a four-station overview on both devices. During a pending call, Intake’s carrier stays visible while LIMEN’s call channel is occupied.

Design handoff

What belongs to the glass

The room

Max, the press, the treadle, both carriers and LIMEN’s voice belong to Conveyor. Their world positions come from the public checkpoint. The proposed planted-foot pose, enlarged die witness and high-contrast treadle edge give one operation a readable silhouette.

The console

The bolted bezel contains the camera title, listening strip and station overview. The local Clear pin targets Forge’s current carrier. Opening the camera selects a subject. A separate press on Clear submits that station’s command. Returning to Wide keeps the clock and station state.

Station control states
ConditionWhat the Producer sees
ReadyA solid diamond in the overview and a filled Clear control at the loaded station.
Call pending“Calling max” at Forge. The press and foot hold their poses. Occupied stations retain a marker with a horizontal bar while LIMEN is busy.
ReceivedThe press travels, max plants his foot and the control reads “Working”. Another ready load can regain Clear once LIMEN is free.
Call missedThe press stays up with C2 in place. Show the station’s missed-call reason and restore Clear when the command becomes available again.
PausedInspection remains available. Disable new station commands and give the existing resume reason. Camera focus never spends a clearance.
See the missed-call alternative

Input and access. Reserve at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels for station selection, Clear and Wide view. Provide named keyboard controls with visible focus. Selection has an outline and a label. Loaded stations have a diamond; empty stations have a dot. Spoken lines remain readable with sound off. A direct camera cut with the same end frame supports reduced motion. No camera transition extends a call or pauses the shift.

For the next handoff. ART would refine max’s foot and head-turn keys and the press contact detail against these crops. ENGINE would bind the selected camera, pending call, receipt, stroke and availability to existing public state. The exported poses and inset remain design studies. Runtime ownership, animation budget, touch verification and the owner’s three-still verdict remain open.
