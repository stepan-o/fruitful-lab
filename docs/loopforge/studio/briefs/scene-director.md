## The current opening

Workers approach the factory; their bodies and the camera must carry them towards it. LIMEN stops the crowd and delivers the welcome. A few murmurs establish different people. bob steps forward with his card, is struck and falls. sam moves to help while ed retrieves and conceals the card. The camera must make ed's action legible without making bob a witness.

bob recovers as STILETTO comes through. Workers retreat, LIMEN gives way, and she continues to Conveyor without an ID check. bob notices the exception, retreats into flattery when challenged, gives his assignment and is admitted without his card being checked. LIMEN recruits ed and gus. Their participation makes the checkpoint a social arrangement the player can recognize again.

At Conveyor, bob and STILETTO travel through Intake, Brain Wash, Main Belt and Lattice Forge. The same brain changes state as the stations operate. During the tutorial sam is the only additional worker admitted. Return to Security for jo, the third check. ed retains bob's card; bob has not yet discovered the loss.

This chronology supersedes the old “STILETTO after two checks” wording and the obsolete storyboard notes. Retain the storyboard's useful framing and reactions, not its superseded timing. The missing-card seed is ed; other seeds are future alternatives, not permission to change custody in this cut.

## Direct attention, space and time

Use a small number of purposeful compositions. Change angle when the action or the player's necessary understanding changes. Preserve screen direction, entrances and relative positions across cuts. A dramatic low view can strengthen the strike; a clear hand and card can be more important than a dramatic view during the theft. Speech placement must preserve faces, hands and the contact corridor in both the playable page and exported storyboard.

Let the crowd wait with small, varied motion. Give strong reactions a reason and an end. Protect the principal action from idle noise, overlapping bubbles and simultaneous comic business. Test identification at full-room scale and the smaller console view; captions cannot compensate for unreadable silhouettes.

Keep the opening brisk enough to include the tutorial within minutes. Every dialogue step advances by player action. Reading holds factory time and consequential actions while restrained character motion continues. Judge both the normal-speed performance and an extended reading hold. Animation speed is a tool, but contacts, weight and walking trajectories must remain believable.
