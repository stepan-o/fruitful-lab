## Morning, shift, aftermath

Each morning LIMEN and STILETTO give one sourced result from yesterday, their response to the player’s actual treatment, and an assignment bid naming a cost they discount. Both initially propose their native rooms. LIMEN values procedure and intact operation; STILETTO wants quota and leadership approval. Selecting an adviser backs judgment; approving assignments determines where it operates. A supervisor cannot describe a private encounter without hearing about it.

During the shift the player uses existing feed/drive controls and eligible incident responses. The adviser acts automatically in their assigned room; elsewhere an authorized decision may belong to the player. A completed automatic act is shown and attributed, never offered for retroactive veto. bob’s inquiry, complaint and willingness to demonstrate remain character acts, not player-selected lines.

Reading holds factory progression, routes, hearing, custody, deadlines and output. Restrained animation continues; hands holding cards or partners keep their fitted pose. Only an enacted wait, stop or programme period costs productive time. A slow reader never causes an accident.

Aftermath first shows a changed working position, a completed cycle or the actual remaining card. Then show the day’s material commitment: retain or dispatch only that day’s completed output, never quietly recycle previously assigned people. The next morning uses that result without a duplicate report. Fatigue recovery cannot repair structural wear, clear custody or erase a promise. Ordinary days still target roughly a minute, heavier days two to three; total pacing is unmeasured. W1’s speech-only estimates omit movement, reading and decisions.
